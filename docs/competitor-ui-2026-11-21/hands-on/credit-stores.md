# Credit and coin stores, hands-on, 2026-11-22

Captured on a real iPhone through iPhone Mirroring for the redesign of
Plotbreak's credits page as a game-style pack store. Nothing was bought. Only
tabs, segmented controls, close buttons and back buttons were tapped; packs were
captured by scrolling. Shots are in `shots/`, 1 px = 1 pt unless the name ends
in `z` (a 2x crop).

## Afizzy: "Get Fizz"

Shots: `afizzy-09-fizz-shop`, `afizzy-09y-grid`, `afizzy-09z-tile`.

- **Entry:** the PRO pill on Home opens a full-screen paywall with a two-option
  segmented control at the top, "Subscription" and "Get Fizz". The packs are the
  second segment, so currency and membership share one screen. A small pink "%"
  badge sits on the Subscription segment.
- **Header:** "My balance:" with a pink gem and the number (331), and a gray
  "No Fizz expiring soon" line under it.
- **Layout:** a 3x2 grid of six tiles, ascending left to right, top to bottom,
  then a "See all" chevron under the grid and a full-width "Get more free Fizz"
  row (the earn path) below it.
- **Packs:** 300 for $0.99 · 1,000 (+100) for $6.99 · 4,000 (+600) for $24.99 ·
  10,000 (+1500) for $49.99 · 25,000 (+5000) for $129.99 · 66,000 for $299.99.
- **Icons:** 3D glossy pink jelly gems. The tier shows in the icon: one gem, two
  gems, then a crystal sundae bowl that fills up and finally overflows. One art
  family, six sizes.
- **Tags:** corner tabs on the top-left of a tile: "New user" (dark) on the
  first pack, "POPULAR" (pink) on 4,000, "MOST VALUE" (orange) on 66,000. The
  bonus is a pink "+N" in bold above the icon, and the 66,000 tile carries a
  faint "First top-up" line where the bonus would sit.
- **Tile:** dark plum card (~#1E1A1E), ~12pt radius, amount in ~22pt bold white,
  price in a full-width pink gradient capsule at the bottom of the tile.
- **Color and type:** plum-to-black gradient behind everything, hot pink as the
  only accent, white SF bold numerals.
- **Game store or not:** yes. The bowl filling up from tier to tier makes the
  bigger packs look bigger, and the corner tabs and "+N" bonus labels read as a
  mobile-game top-up screen.

## Character AI: Charms

Shots: `cai-store-01` to `09`. Crops: `01z-dice`, `01y-spend-card`,
`02z-daily-login`, `05z-tile`, `05y-banners`, `05x-icon-368`, `06z/06x/06y`
(pack icons, rows 1 to 3), `07z-plan-card`.

Three screens: a Charms wallet, a "Get more Charms" pack store, and the c.ai+
membership paywall.

**1. Charms wallet** (`cai-store-01` to `04`)
- **Entry:** the "C" square icon in the Home header, right side, next to the
  bell. It opens a pushed page titled "Charms" with back, (i), a receipt icon
  (history) and a headset (support).
- **Top:** one big 3D blue translucent letter cube with a white "C" on its faces,
  floating, then the balance as a "C" chip plus "0" in ~30pt, then a white
  capsule "(+) Buy Charms".
- **Below:** a "How to spend your Charms" card (check list: skip ads, boost
  through slowmode, extra Imagines, more coming), with a purple speckled cube
  cropped off the card's right edge. Then a "1 Hour / No Ads" card (white
  headline, blue second line) with a stack of four cubes (pink, star, blue,
  heart) as a totem, and "Get pass for C 20".
- **Earn section:** a "Daily log in" card in a blue gradient with a cube icon,
  "X5" and a white "Claim" capsule; "Watch your first ad, get 5 Charms" with a
  segmented 0-of-5 progress bar; then "Starter Quests" (a dark pill with a blue
  dot): Free 20 Charms, Create a Persona (X10), Enable notifications (X15), Add
  an intro video (X5), Imagine a Chat Image (X10), Post to the Feed (X10).
  Claimable quests are blue gradient cards; the rest are dark gray. Every row
  uses the same cube icon in an outlined square with the reward as "XN" under

**2. Get more Charms** (`cai-store-05`, `06`)
- **Entry:** it opened as a page pushed on top of the Charms wallet. I never
  tapped "Buy Charms"; it appeared after a back tap from the wallet and went back
  to the wallet on the next back. It is very likely the same screen "Buy Charms"
  opens.
- **Header:** back, a balance pill "C 0", (i), receipt. Title "Get more Charms"
  in ~30pt bold white, subtitle "Unlock more. Spend as you go." in gray.
- **Two banners** above the grid: a magenta-to-pink gradient "Get up to 50%
  extra Charms! The larger the pack, the bigger your bonus." with fine print
  "First-purchase bonus applies to one purchase per account" and a cube pile
  (heart, pink, green) on its right edge; then a solid royal-blue "Get 20% more
  Charms with (c.ai+), every purchase" with a white "Try (c.ai+)" capsule.
- **Layout:** a 2-column grid of six tiles, three rows, ascending.
- **Packs** (new amount, old amount struck through, bonus pill, price):
  110 ~~100~~ 10% first-time bonus $0.99 · 368 ~~300~~ 15% $3.99 ·
  1,356 ~~1,000~~ 20% $12.99 · 3,738 ~~2,500~~ 30% $34.99 ·
  8,470 ~~5,000~~ 40% $69.99 · 18,750 ~~10,000~~ 50% $129.99.
- **Icons:** 3D glossy translucent letter cubes, like plastic alphabet beads,
  with "C", hearts and stars on the faces. They scale by count: one blue cube,
  then a blue pair with a pink one, a small cluster, a row of five, a stacked
  pyramid of ~12, and a heap of ~25 in every color. The icon sits top-left in
  the tile, not centered.
- **Tile:** dark gray card (~#1F1F1F), ~14pt radius, an (i) glyph top-right,
  amount in ~20pt bold white with a small "C" chip before it and the old amount
  in gray strikethrough after it, a magenta pill "N% first-time bonus", and a
  small white price capsule bottom-left with black text. No best-value or

- **Color and type:** near-black page, white bold numerals, magenta for bonus,
  royal blue for membership. SF-style sans throughout.

**3. c.ai+ membership** (`cai-store-07` to `09`)
- **Entry:** the white "Upgrade" pill next to the (c.ai) logo on Home.
- A full-bleed holographic photo (a woman with a giant rainbow-sheen rat) over
  the top third, X top-left, "Restore" top-right.
- A plan card with a segmented control "(c.ai) lite" / "(c.ai+)". **The whole
  screen recolors with the plan:** c.ai+ is royal blue (outline, toggle, table
  column, Subscribe button), lite is lime green with black text.
- c.ai+: ~~$155.88~~ $129.99 per year, Monthly/Yearly toggle, "save 17%" pink
  pill. Lite: ~~$13.00~~ $6.99 per month, Weekly/Monthly toggle, "save 46%".
  Four check-marked perks per plan.
- "All the good stuff": a Free / Lite / c.ai+ comparison table (ads, chat styles
  PipSqueak/DeepSqueak/LongSqueak, memory, pins 15/30/30, lorebook, limits, slow

**Game store or not:** the pack store, yes. The six icons are one toy family
that piles up, every tile shows a struck-through base amount and a growing
bonus, and the wallet page has quests, a daily login and XN reward slots, which
is a mobile-game economy screen. The membership paywall is a regular SaaS
comparison table, but the plan-colored reskin makes switching plans feel like
picking a team.

- **Header:** a full-screen sheet with an X top-left, a warm amber light beam
  from the top-right corner onto a black page, and the mascot (a flat-shaded

  Images (photos sent by characters), Voices.
- **Layout:** a 2-column grid of six tiles plus a seventh full-width "hero" row

  1,000 (+250) $12.99 · 2,000 (+500) $24.99 · 3,000 (+900) $39.99 ·

## Ranking, by how good the store looks

1. **Character AI, Get more Charms.** One toy-like 3D cube family that piles up
   tier by tier, a struck-through base amount and a magenta "N% first-time
   bonus" on every tile, a promo banner that explains the bonus curve, and a
   wallet page with quests and a daily login. The best game-store read.
2. **Afizzy, Get Fizz.** Same idea with jelly gems filling a crystal bowl, plus
   corner tabs (New user, POPULAR, MOST VALUE) and "+N" bonuses. Loud pink, but
   it reads instantly as a top-up shop.
3. **Swerve, Gems.** Growing 3D crystals and gradient price pills in a real shop
   tab with earn rows, quests and cosmetics. Loses points for no bonus or value
   tags and for putting the packs last.
4. **EMOCHI, Sparks.** Strong header (mascot in a light beam), a glowing hero
   row for the top pack, countdowns and a 2x event, but every tile uses the same
   tiny glyph, so the tiers look alike.
5. **OOC, My Credit.** Clean radio list with one Buy button and a "Popular" tag.
   Reads as a utility top-up.
6. **Plotbreak, My credits.** Has the right offer data (first purchase, popular,
   best value, bonuses, turns per pack) but in a settings-style radio list with
   no art, and the Pass card fills the first screen so only one pack shows above
   the fold.

**What the top three share:**
1. A single currency object drawn in 3D that multiplies with the tier (cube
   pile, gem bowl, crystal cluster), so a bigger pack looks bigger without
   reading the number.
2. A 2- or 3-column grid of tiles, each with its own colored price pill at the
   bottom, instead of a radio list with one shared Buy button.
3. Bonus labeling on every tile: "+N" or a struck-through base amount,
   first-purchase badges and corner tabs, backed by an earn or quest section
   (daily login, quests) on the same page.
