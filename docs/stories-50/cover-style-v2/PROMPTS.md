# Cover style v2: official new-50 covers (2026-11-22)

Why: next to OOC's shelf (`docs/ooc-app-2026-11-21/shots/12-new-ranking.png`) our covers read modest
and flat. Theirs sell on one very attractive adult lead, large in frame, with some skin and a confident pose.

**What changed.** `generatedCoverPrompt` (`packages/director/src/media/prompts.ts`), only when the
`coverDirection` has a `TITLE:` line (official covers): the mild `CAST_APPEAL` line is replaced by
`OFFICIAL_COVER_APPEAL` + `OFFICIAL_COVER_LIMITS`, followed by the unchanged child/teen ABSOLUTE RULE, and
the "Avoid: gratuitous cleavage, fanservice framing" negative is dropped. Creator covers (`create-art.ts`,
no `TITLE:` line) get a byte-identical prompt, pinned by sha256 in
`packages/director/src/media/cover-prompt.spec.ts`.

**The style.** Commercial gacha/Webtoon key art. The lead is a glamorous adult with good proportions, large
in frame, in a confident, teasing or dynamic pose. Outfits are form-fitting with some skin where the story
allows it: bare shoulders, a neckline with some cleavage, midriff, a thigh-high slit, a bodysuit, an
off-shoulder kimono. A male lead gets an open collar, a fitted shirt, a sharp jawline and a confident look.

**Hard limits (in every official prompt, and asserted in each story's spec).** App rated 13+.
- Every character is clearly an adult, said in words: "a grown woman who looks in her late twenties",
  "a man in his early thirties", a mature face and an adult build. Never a childlike face or body.
- No nudity, nipples, underwear or lingerie as the outfit, sexual acts or sexual poses (no spread legs,
  no lying down). Swimwear only for a beach or pool story.
- School stories: no sexualized uniforms; the appeal comes from adult styling. The ABSOLUTE RULE for
  child and teen characters is untouched, and `nsfw, nude, child` stay in PixAI's `DEFAULT_NEGATIVE`.
- This reverses PIPELINE.md's "never by age words" for the lead: adult age words are now required.

**Test stories** (none is a school story; every cast member is written as an adult):
- Married to the Shrine Fox: Kuzuha, crimson robe off both shoulders, white kimono with a low neckline, thigh slit, legs crossed, teasing.
- The One-Copper Inn: Ember, cloak off one shoulder, fitted sleeveless wrap top, bare midriff, rib bandage, leaning on the counter.
- The Maid Owns the House Now: Clara, fitted black off-shoulder dress, sweetheart neckline, thigh slit, hip on the banister, apron dangled like a dare.
- Seven Years Early: Victor, fitted white shirt open to the third button, coat over his shoulders, sharp jaw, hand out to shake.

**Generate just these 4 into this folder** (Tsubaki.3 pro, 1152x1744, ~4,400 credits each, ~17.6K total;
never writes `infra/seed/assets/*/cover.*` or the manifest; ledger label `cover-style-v2:<storyId>`):

    npx tsx --env-file=.env docs/stories-50/tools/cover-style-v2.mts --generate            # all 4
    npx tsx --env-file=.env docs/stories-50/tools/cover-style-v2.mts --generate --only=story_seven_years_early
    npx tsx docs/stories-50/tools/cover-style-v2.mts > /tmp/p.md                           # reprint prompts, no API
