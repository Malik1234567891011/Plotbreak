# Cover titles v4: one title design per story

2026-11-23. One line per story, in shelf order (sorted by title). Each line is the brief
for that story's `titles/<storyId>.json` spec (format: `tools/cover-title-v4.py`).

**Why.** The owner on the v3 finals: "I love the images but the fonts are repetitive, the
ones with the same art style have the same font." On the six v4 samples: "they're good
apart from the shrine fox, can't really read it sideways." So:

- Every title reads left to right. No vertical or sideways text, no stacked columns of
  letters. Tilts up to 12 deg are fine. Treatment 6 in `OOC-COVERS.md` (vertical side
  column) is retired.
- The five samples he liked are kept as they are: Gallows, Skill: ERROR, Finals Week
  Outbreak, Ice Queen, Wyvern Slayer. Shrine Fox keeps its fonts but is set horizontally,
  bottom right.
- Every story gets its own primary family (50 different ones from `titles/fonts.json`).

**Rules,** all checked by `python3 -I docs/stories-50/tools/check-title-allocation.py`:

1. 50 different primary families; every family is in `fonts.json` (OFL).
2. No family on more than 3 covers, counting accents.
3. No family on two covers that share a v3 recipe (the same art style).
4. Shelf neighbours share no family at all and never the same placement.
5. No placement used more than 9 times (8 until the art director moved Every Platform's departure board to the bottom; BT is the one code at 9).
6. Every title zone stays clear of every face box.
7. No vertical or sideways title, no tilt over 12 deg.
8. Each treatment is used once.
9. Title text is the fixture title exactly.

**Placement codes.** TB top band. BT bottom third, centred. UL / UR upper-left /
upper-right block. LL / LR lower-left / lower-right block. DG slight diagonal (6-12 deg).
CG centred over a gap in the art. CW corner logo plus a giant word. WR wrapped around the
figure (the words sit either side of it).

**Zone and Face** are fractions of the 1152x1744 canvas, `x0-x1,y0-y1`, measured on the
raws with the v4 centred crop and checked on an overlay of every cover. Zone is where the
title ink goes; Face boxes become the spec's `avoid` (multiply by 1152 and 1744). They are
eyeballed, so keep a margin and run `--debug` on each render.

**Hero word.** Each brief names one hero word, at least twice the cap height of the rest.
