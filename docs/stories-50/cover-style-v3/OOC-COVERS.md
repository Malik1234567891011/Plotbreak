# OOC cover variety: what the shelf actually looks like

Catalogued 2026-11-22 to answer the owner's complaint about v2: "OOC has many
different art styles and different types of covers, they're not all the same."
He is right. Our 50 v2 covers are all one look. On OOC that look makes up
about 17% of the shelf.

## Sources and method

- **Live web shelf, https://ooc.ai, logged in, 2026-11-22.** Every story cover
  on the home page, deduplicated: Top Ranked (20), Popular OOC Originals (20),
  New OOC Originals (15), Trending Now (10), Trending New Creators (14), New
  from certified creators (15) and the first 50 of Explore (Most Played). That
  comes to **108 unique covers**. Each was viewed at about 250x375 in a contact
  sheet built inside the tab, 12 at a time. Read-only: nothing was clicked,
  liked or downloaded.
- **App screenshots** in `docs/ooc-app-2026-11-21/shots/` (01, 03, 12, 13, 64,
  77). Shot 77 (Rankings > Female-Oriented) shows a look that never appears on
  the web home shelf. It is cluster 12 below.
- **Character tab** (ooc.ai/character): 16 user-made character cards sampled.
  They are not counted in the 108 because they are user art, not curated
  covers. Notes are at the end.
- The web home has no Rankings or genre-row pills (`/ranking` is a 404). Genre
  rows exist only in the app.

## The single most important finding

**No two neighbours on a shelf row share a look.** Here are the first 12 of
Top Ranked, in order:

1. Holy white and silver
2. Warm kitchen
3. Light-novel ensemble on blue sky
4. Purple close-up with a giant sans title
5. Yellow lightning esports
6. Pale silver
7. Gold-and-purple duo, "Vol. 4"
8. Sunny school trio
9. Ice-blue action crouch
10. Stadium daylight
11. Red and black with a katana
12. Black-and-white ink

New OOC Originals runs the same way: B&W ink, hot pink, gold cathedral, green
field, red/gold back view, blue cosmos, orange/purple split, white/gold angel,
muted bokeh, dim blue, black with a pink rim, gold frame, starfield, beige soft
light, purple idol group.

Three things change from one cover to the next:

1. **Value.** Light covers sit beside dark ones. About 30% of the 108 read as
   light or bright overall (high-key white, daylight sky, hot pink, cream).
   v2 has none.
2. **Dominant hue.** About ten families are in rotation (listed under
   "Palette spread" below).
3. **Title voice.** A 30%-height block sans sits beside a thin script, then
   engraved gold, then a brush, then a game logo.

A second finding: **OOC serves different cover art for the same story.** The
app shots from 2026-11-21 show three cases:

| Story | App (shots 12/13) | Web (2026-11-22) |
|---|---|---|
| A Trash Noble's Academy Survival | A dark-haired young man in a noble uniform | A dark-haired woman in a navy and gold uniform, hands on hips |
| I Became the Oyabun | A woman facing front in a gold kimono | The back view with a dragon tattoo |
| War of Heaven and Earth | A dark-haired woman in purple | A blonde angel in gold armour |

The title lockup stays the same in each pair. Only the art changes, so this
is either rotation or targeting by audience.

## What this means for our 50

1. **Give no look more than about 6 of the 50**, and use 10-12 looks. Dark fantasy glow, which is all of v2 today, should be about 8 at most.
2. **Alternate value and hue along each shelf row.** About a third of the covers should be light: high-key, daylight, hot pink or cream.
3. **Vary composition**, roughly 45% solo waist-up, about 20% two or more characters, plus back views, profiles, close-ups, full body and one or two with no character.
4. **The title is part of the look.** We need at least 5 title voices, not one Cinzel. Google font candidates for `cover-title.py`:
   - Condensed heavy: Anton, Bebas Neue, Oswald
   - Esports italic: Russo One, Teko
   - Rounded heavy: Fredoka, Baloo 2
   - Engraved serif: Cinzel Decorative, Cormorant SC
   - Swash script: Pinyon Script, Great Vibes, Playball
   - Brush: Permanent Marker, Yuji Syuku
   - Tech: Orbitron, VT323
   - Two-colour accent words and a top-placed title for drama.
5. **13+ adaptation.** Copy the look, not OOC's content. Several OOC covers we
   must not mirror:
   - School-uniform girls (My High School Romance, She Has Her Eyes on Me,
     Summoned Into a Girls' Dorm, Merit Supremacy, OOC High). Get school appeal
     from styling and expression instead.
   - Children (The Family We Found).
   - Chest-framed poses (My Ex Moved In, Sharing an Apartment).

   Back views, gowns, kimono off the shoulder, armour, and evening and stage
   wear carry the appeal within our limits.

## Title logos (2026-11-23)

Added for v4 titles. The owner's complaint about v3: covers that share a recipe share
a font, so "the ones with the same art style have the same font". On OOC no two titles
are set alike, even inside one look. This section looks at the title as a **logo**
rather than as a type choice. Sources: the app shots (01, 03, 06, 12, 13, 35, 64, 77,
ooc2-01z, ooc2-04z) enlarged 2x, plus the 37 detailed records above. The web shelf
was not reopened for this pass.

### What OOC's logos have in common

- **Every logo mixes at least two voices.** A small kicker in one face and a big word
  in another (OYABUN, HUMAN, AGAIN, APEX). Fewer than 1 in 10 use one font at one size.
- **One word carries the logo.** The hero word is 2-4x the cap height of the rest:
  "Burned" in The World I Burned to Make You Mine, "AGAIN", "HUMAN", "OYABUN",
  "ACADEMY" in Trash Noble's. The small words are tucked around it, never stacked as
  equals.
- **Colour comes from the art.** HUMAN is the background pink, Oyabun's cream comes
  from the kimono, Hunter Academy's silver from the lightning, and Lumencia's
  lavender-white from the halo. A title colour that appears nowhere in the art is rare.
- **Position follows the empty space, not a template.** Bottom third is most common,
  but Reincarnated OP Skill and Psychic Academy NOVA sit at the top, Vampire in Korea
  and LOOKISM run down the side, The Hero Who Came to Kill Me is set on a diagonal,
  and The Last Human is a small top-right corner logo.
- **Finish is part of the genre.** Engraved metal for nobility, a bevelled 3D gold for
  games, white-and-outline stickers for comedy, neon tubes for city nights, a brush and
  a red seal for East Asian settings, distress for horror.

### Rules for the 50

1. **No font family on more than 3 covers**, and never on two covers that share a
   look (a v3 recipe). The library has 60 families, so this is easy to keep.
2. **Each cover gets a hero word** at least 2x the cap height of the rest of the
   title. A title without an obvious hero word ("Three Feet Apart") picks the most
   visual one ("APART").
3. **Sample the title colour from the art** (`fill: {"sample": [x, y]}`) or pick a
   complement of the art's dominant hue, then check it at shelf size (160 px wide).
4. **Place the title in the art's empty space**, with every face boxed in `avoid`. At
   least 8 of the 50 go top, 4 go vertical or diagonal, and 4 are small corner logos.
5. **One genre finish per cover** (metal, sticker, neon, brush, distress, glitch,
   engraved, paper). Do not stack two finishes on one word.
