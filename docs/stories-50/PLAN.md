## 4. Per-story pipeline

### One-time setup before story 1 (about 1.5 days of engineering)

1. **PixAI in the art pipeline.** Add a provider switch to `infra/scripts/generate-art.ts`:
   - Covers go to Tsubaki.3 with the title painted in, so the `cover-title.ts` composite is skipped for PixAI covers.
   - Key art, portraits and location stages go to Flash.
   - Keep the gpt-4.1-mini title read-back, and add the franchise-symbol check the bake-off asked for.
   - The tested call is in `tools/pixai-one-cover.mjs`.
2. **Opening panel.** `opening-panel-art.ts` draws the panel from the character's portrait with an
   image-edit call, so the face matches. Check whether PixAI takes a reference image. If not, keep
   gpt-image for that one image per story (about $0.04).
3. **Local database for migrate and smoke.** The root `.env` holds only the PixAI key today. Point
   `migrate` at a local Postgres with `--url=`. Never run it against production in this phase.
4. **Story kit.** A bible template, an empty fixture and spec scaffolded from Good Morning, Husband (our
   best-performing structure), and the three checks from `stories-checkpoint.md` as one script:
   - gated abilities must be granted somewhere;
   - every `cardBlurb` must contain "you";
   - every location needs an inbound edge.

the user decided.

side by side. The limit on the user's Claude plan, not money, is what caps throughput.

## 7. Quality bar

A story ships only when all of these hold:

1. **The opener.**
   - A named character speaks to the player within the first 60 words.
   - The beat ends on a direct question or a demand that needs an answer now.
   - At most 120 words, with no proper noun the player must already know.
   - The three suggestions are three different moves: one said, one done, one risky.
2. **Adults only.** Every character's age is 18 or over and stated in the bible. Academies are
   post-secondary. Covers show adults only, described by costume and pose, never by age words. That
   is the `generate-art.ts` lesson from Ace's refused cover.
3. **Original.**
   - No OOC title, character or premise copied word for word.
   - No franchise names, symbols or terms (Itachi's cover drew the village headband: the franchise-symbol check exists for that).
   - A collision search on the title.
4. **Gates green.**
   - The three checks pass.
   - `npm test` and `npm run typecheck` pass; they are separate gates.
   - The clarity validator raises no FAIL.
   - `npm run smoke -- --only=<slug>` exits 0. Smoke plays the world badly on purpose: attacks, lies, refusals, misspelled names.
5. **Cover.**
   - Title spelled exactly and legible at shelf-thumbnail size.
   - No stray text: the Flash stray-mark problem.
   - Face readable at thumbnail size.
   - Passes the read-back and the franchise-symbol checks.
6. **One real transcript.** The reviewer reads the first ten beats of one local session, via `npm run playtest`.
   - Do the cast sound different from each other?
   - Does turn 1 change something?
   - Does beat 10 still feel like the same story?
7. **After launch.**
   - Target: at least 55% of first runs take a turn, measured after 30 first runs. Our good openers run 50–67%, the weak ones 37–43%.
   - Target: no more than 50% of first runs stop by turn 3.
