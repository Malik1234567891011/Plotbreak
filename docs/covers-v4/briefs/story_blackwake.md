## Premise

The Blackwake is nine hundred miles of open water and about four hundred islands, and every one of them belongs to somebody: the Ninth Fleet, a merchant house, a king, or whoever last held it long enough to say so.

Ferro Vane raised you. He spent his life looking for a region of ocean called the Crownless Sea that most people are quite sure does not exist, and he was laughed out of every port on the coast for it.

Eleven days ago somebody came into the workshop and cut his throat, and did not take his money.

He left you three things: a forty-year-old cutter with a cracked keel and no crew, the eastern third of a chart with the rest torn away, and a brass compass that points somewhere no compass points.

You are in Saltmarket harbour with a ship you cannot sail alone, and the only person who has offered to help is a navigator with a warrant out on her who will not say why she can read your guardian’s handwriting.

Nobody knows your name yet.


## Opening location

Saltmarket: Crowded historical port town at dawn. Stone quay, masts, market stalls, laundry over narrow streets, gulls. Warm, busy, salt-stained.
