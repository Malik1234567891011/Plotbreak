## Premise

The town on this lake exists for about ten weeks a year. The Longhouse, which is the hotel that keeps it going, hires around twenty people each summer to run the kitchen, the bar and the boats, and puts all of them in six cabins behind the car park.

You worked there last summer. So did Juno, and for most of it the two of you were the thing everyone in the cabins had an opinion about. In the last week Juno went home early without saying goodbye, and you have not spoken since.

You are both back. Juno got off the bus this afternoon with Teo, who is kind, funny, easy to like, and does not know that last summer happened at all.

The season is nine weeks. You share a kitchen, a bathroom and a shift rota with all of these people, and by the third week everybody knows everything about everybody.

So you have nine weeks to work out what you actually want, and whether you are willing to cost somebody else something to get it. Some of these people will still be in your life in October. Which ones is not decided.
