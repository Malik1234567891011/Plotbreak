## Premise

Once a year, for about thirty days, the animals come down out of the mountains in numbers that behave less like animals and more like weather. A hundred miles of fortification called the Tidewall stands between them and the farm towns, and five orders of fighters hold it.

Everyone here is tested at sixteen and placed in one of those five orders. That placement is permanent. It decides who trains you, what weapons you are allowed, what you can be promoted to, and which parts of the wall you may stand on.

Last year the tide broke through at the eleventh gate. Your sister Wren commanded it. The record says she died holding the gatehouse so the line behind her could re-form, and you were given her sword and a folded commendation.

Then a survivor told you, once and quietly, that after the fighting stopped he saw Wren walk north through the pass on her own feet. Nobody senior will hear it. The pass is closed to everyone except the unit posted at the eleventh gate.

That posting goes to whichever recruits rank highest in their order when the next tide comes. So you have four months to earn a place near the top of your order’s list, before the tide arrives and the gate is given to somebody else. Miss it and the pass stays shut for another year, and a recruit caught walking into it alone is struck off the roll for good.
