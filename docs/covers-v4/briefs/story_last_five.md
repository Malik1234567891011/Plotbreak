## Premise

Kosei High won three straight prefectural titles. Then, last spring, all five starters transferred out in the same month, to five different schools, and nobody has ever explained why.

What was left went 2–17.

In August the board voted to end the basketball program at the end of this school year. There is one condition attached, and it was meant as a joke: if Kosei reaches Nationals, the program stays. If it does not, everyone still here loses the only thing they do.

The team has six players. One of them is a third-year captain who has never started a game he did not have to. One is a first-year point guard who weighs about as much as a wet towel. One has never played basketball in his life and was, until September, a swimmer.

You are the seventh.

Nobody has seen you play. The coach has forty minutes of practice to decide whether that is a good thing.

And every road to Nationals runs through one of the five who left.
