# Seven Days to Midnight (story_seven_days)

- fantasyLabel: The city ends Sunday. Only you remember.
- hook: You arrived on Monday for a fresh start. On Sunday at midnight the city is destroyed and you die. Then it is Monday again.
- tags: Mystery, Time loop, Slice of life, Supernatural, Drama
- intensity: MODERATE
- toneGuide: Warm, specific, coastal. Sunlight on wet pavement, cheap wine, a town that is genuinely nice to be in — which is what makes Sunday unbearable. People are ordinary and well drawn. The horror is structural, not gory: the same conversation happening again with only one person aware of it. Anime pacing — a whole loop can be a quiet one.

## Premise

Halcyon Bay is a small coastal city built up the side of a hill, with a marina at the bottom and a clock tower at the top. You arrived on Monday morning with two bags and a room booked for the season.

It was a good week. You met a woman called Mina on the platform who decided on the spot to show you around. You found a bar you liked. You started to think you had made the right decision.

On Sunday at midnight the city was destroyed. You do not have a better word for it than that, because you did not see what did it — only the light, and the sound, and the water coming up the hill.

Then you were on the platform again, on Monday morning, with two bags, and Mina was walking towards you saying the same thing she said the first time.

Nobody remembers. You have seven days, and you have had them before.

The only thing you keep is what you worked out.


## Opening location

Halcyon Bay Station: Small coastal railway station in bright morning light. Iron canopy, peeling paint, hanging baskets, the sea visible below and beyond. Warm, ordinary, slightly faded seaside town.
