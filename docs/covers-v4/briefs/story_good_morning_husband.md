## Premise

You go to sleep in your own life and wake up in this one. Same body, same name, same memory of being a child. What is missing is the last several years, and in those years you apparently got married.

There is a gold band on your left hand that does not come off easily, because it has been there long enough for your finger to have grown around it.

There is a framed photograph on the dresser of you and a woman outside a registry office, both laughing at something outside the frame. Her name is Hana Mori. The calendar in the hall says four years.

Nothing dramatic happened last night. No accident, no hospital, no bang on the head. You simply went to sleep somewhere else.

She does not know anything is wrong. When you tell her she will assume you are joking, because that is the kind of thing the two of you do to each other.

So you have a marriage you did not build, a flat full of evidence about a man you have to take on trust, and a wife who will notice within a day that you are reading her like a stranger.

What you do about that is yours. Tell her. Play along until it stops being playing. Keep this life and make it better than the one you lost, or go and find out what happened and lose this one working it out.

She has a secret of her own, and hers has a deadline.
