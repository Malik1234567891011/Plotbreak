# Red Moon Brigade (story_red_moon)

- fantasyLabel: You hunt monsters. You are becoming one.
- hook: Your first real hunt killed your squad and should have killed you. Something in the Behemoth decided otherwise.
- tags: Monsters, Military, Dark fantasy, Rivalry, Body horror
- intensity: INTENSE
- toneGuide: Military, physical, close. People are tired and competent and make jokes about things that are not funny. Behemoths are animals, not demons — enormous, indifferent, and horrifying because of scale rather than malice. Body horror is specific and brief: what a hand does, not a paragraph about flesh. Anime pacing — a quiet scene in the armoury earns the next one on the wall.

## Premise

People live inside walls because of the Behemoths — animals the size of buildings that come out of the wild country and do not stop for anything a wall is made of. Fort Ember is one of eleven cities left. The Brigade is what stands between it and them.

You were eight when one came through your town. You have wanted this since.

Four days ago you went out on your first real hunt with six other people and came back alone. The official record says the Behemoth was killed and that you survived by luck.

What actually happened is that it had you in its mouth, and something in its chest went into you instead. You woke up under it with your ribs whole and your hands doing something hands do not do.

Since then you can hold a plate of Behemoth shell across your forearm for about nine seconds. It is getting easier, which is the part that frightens you.

The Brigade has a standing order about people who come back changed. Nobody will tell you what it is. Dr Voss has asked to examine you twice. Captain Venn has not asked anything, which is worse.

You are back on the roster tomorrow.


## Opening location

Fort Ember: Interior of a huge stone fortification built into a city wall. Long galleries, hanging lamps, racked weapons, uniformed people moving with purpose. Warm lamplight against cold grey stone. Overcast light through arrow slits.
