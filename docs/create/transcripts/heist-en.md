### The Door Beneath Rue des Martyrs
*Gaslamp Heist Thriller*

In 1908 Paris, reformed locksmith Élodie Varenne must crack an impossible lock before a police prefect uses its secret to bury her imprisoned brother forever.

**Premise**

Paris, 1908: Élodie Varenne earns an honest living repairing safes, apartment locks, and jammed shop shutters, refusing every invitation from the burglary crew she abandoned three years ago. Then Marcel Duret, her former fence, brings word of a door sealed beneath an abandoned printing house on Rue des Martyrs. Its lock bears the private maker's mark of Prefect Augustin Ravel, the celebrated policeman whose testimony sent Élodie's brother Luc to Saint-Martin Prison. Behind the door lies Ravel's private archive: records capable of proving Luc was framed, or exposing enough powerful people to start a quiet war across the city. Élodie's old crew wants the archive sold. Ravel's agents want it erased. Luc's prison transfer order has already been signed, though nobody will say where he is being sent. Every key, favor, and alleyway in Montmartre has a price, and Élodie knows the one rule of her former life: a door is never just a door; it is a promise about what waits on the other side.

> You work by feel beneath the shelter of your coat. The third lever hangs a fraction low; the fifth needs less lift than its spring pretends. One by one, the pins settle.
