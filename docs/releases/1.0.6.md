# 1.0.6 (build 14)

App Store "What's New" copy. Source commits: `82f643e`, `550e9c1`, `33c4a4a`,
and `7e46ae2`.

Most of this release is invisible. The one new thing on screen only appears to
players several hundred turns into a single story, which today is a handful of
people — so the copy leads with the writing instead.

## English

Arcs. A story you have been playing for a long time now marks the end of an
arc, with a recap of everything that led there. It arrives at a pause the story
chooses, never in the middle of a scene.

Better writing at depth. Characters no longer talk you out of moments you went
looking for, and a long romance can move at the pace you are actually playing
it.

Fixes. Suggested replies can no longer arrive garbled.

## App Review notes

Nothing in this build changes pricing, purchases or the credit economy. The
products, the two offers and the server-side windows are exactly as reviewed
for 1.0.5.

**Arcs** are a performance and continuity feature. A long story re-sends its
whole history to the model on every turn; past a certain length that is both
slow and expensive, so the story is periodically compacted into a recap. The
recap is shown to the player as an "Arc" card in their story feed. It is not a
purchase, not a gate, and not a limit on how long a story can run — a player
can keep playing indefinitely either way. Nothing the player has written is
deleted.

**Writing changes.** Guidance to the storytelling model was revised in two
places: intimate scenes now close the way fiction normally closes them rather
than being refused in dialogue by a character, and the model no longer
de-escalates a tense moment the player deliberately walked into. The app
remains non-explicit; what happens between scenes is never depicted.
