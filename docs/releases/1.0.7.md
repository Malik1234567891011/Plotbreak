## App Review notes

**Plotbreak Pass (`pass_weekly`)** is an auto-renewable weekly subscription.
While it is active our server grants 400 credits once per day (00:00 UTC): on
the purchase itself, and after that each time the player taps the daily-credits
row in the wallet. It also unlocks the Story requests board (submit one request
a week, vote on others).

To reach it: open the app, tap the credit balance at the top right of Discover,
and the Pass is the first card on the Credit packs tab. It also appears on the
"Keep playing" sheet shown when a player runs out of credits. The price, the
weekly period, the auto-renewal terms, how to cancel, and links to the Terms of
Use and Privacy Policy are shown on the card before the purchase button is
pressed. Active subscribers see "Manage subscription", which opens Apple's own
subscription sheet.

Credits remain consumable and are only granted by our server after Apple
confirms the transaction. The $9.99 limited-time offer now opens only for an
account that has already bought something, and its window is 72 hours,
recorded server-side as before.
