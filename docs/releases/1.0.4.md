## English

Come say hi. Join the Plotbreak Discord, post the code from your Badges, and
collect 250 credits.

## Français

Viens dire bonjour. Rejoins le Discord de Plotbreak, poste le code de tes
badges et récupère 250 crédits.

## App Review notes

The "Say Hi on Discord" badge is optional and nothing in the app is locked
behind it. Players who choose to join our community Discord post a code there;
our bot confirms it and the badge's credits can then be collected in-app. It
sits alongside the other badges, which reward playing.
