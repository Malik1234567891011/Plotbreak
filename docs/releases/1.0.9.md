# 1.0.9 (build 17): Relics

Relics: collectible items that drop from story moments in Pink Tide and Second
Skin, a Relic Vault on the profile tab, and 7 relic badges. See `docs/relics.md`.

## Why 1.0.9 and not 1.0.8 (16)

Relics were committed as 1.0.8 (16), but 1.0.8 (16) had already shipped on
2026-11-07 as a screenshot-only build (see `1.0.8.md`). By 2026-11-09 it was
"Ready for Distribution". Build 16 was used up, and the 1.0.8 binary in players'
hands has no relic code.

The server showed relic badges to app version 1.0.8 and up
(`clientShowsRelics`), so for about 10 minutes after `PLOTBREAK_RELICS=all`
went live, 1.0.8 players could get relic badges with no art and no vault. The
cutoff is now 1.0.9 (`6ad7c37`), deployed on Railway at 02:25 EST.
