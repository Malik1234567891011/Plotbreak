## App Review notes

**Credits and the two offers.** Credits are consumable and are spent per story
turn. Two offers sit above the five standing packs:

- **First purchase** — the $0.99 pack (`crd_starter_700`) grants double credits
  the first time an account buys anything. The bonus is applied server-side from
  the account's own purchase history; it is not a separate product, and it does
  not expire.
- **Limited time** — the $9.99 pack (`crd_8200`) grants double credits while a
  twelve-hour window is open. **The window is real.** It opens only when a
  player runs out of credits partway through a story, it is recorded as a row in
  our append-only ledger and the deadline is computed from that row on our
  server, so it cannot be restarted by reinstalling the app or changing the
  device clock. It cannot reopen for seven days. When it lapses the price
  returns to normal.

No countdown is shown that is not backed by that server-side deadline, and the
free routes — daily credits, and credits earned from badges — are shown on the
same screen as the offer rather than hidden behind it.

**Character setup is now optional.** Starting a story creates a sensible default
identity; the customization screen is still available from the story page and
from inside a run.
