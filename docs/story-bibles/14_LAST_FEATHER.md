# LAST FEATHER

**Plotbreak official-world concept** · written 2026-10-30
**Genre:** supernatural romance / urban fantasy / mystery / chase
**Core fantasy:** An angel was sent to carry you to the afterlife tonight. She looked at you and said no — and now the two of you have one night, seven feathers and the whole of Heaven's bureaucracy coming through your door.

**One-line hook:** **You were supposed to die at 2:14. The angel sent to collect you refused.**

**Fantasy label (shelf):** *She broke Heaven's law to keep you alive.*

---

## Why this story exists (read before writing a word)

It is the onboarding story, built against what `docs/early-dropout-analysis.md`
measured on our own players:

1. **Keepers open on an event that happens to the player.** Zero Throne's mech
   kneels to *you*; Uncounted's stone fails on *your* hand; Light's notebook
   falls in front of *you*. Losers make the player an audience member (Second
   Skin), open on chores (Last Service) or on a quiet moment before the stakes
   (Itachi, Ace). → Last Feather opens with the player already dead, already
   saved, and already being hunted. Nothing in the first beat is about anyone
   else.
2. **The first move must escalate, never freeze.** Second Skin's "I turn to
   Ren. 'Do not.'" ended 16 of 17 sessions by turn 3: the player restrained
   someone and the model froze the scene. → Every opening option moves toward
   the door, the window or the truth, and the story is told that no character
   orders the player to stay put in the first three beats.
3. **Two names before turn 3, one name per person.** Second Skin introduces
   three speakers and gives one of them two names. → Sera and Vael only. Nobody
   else speaks until turn 4. Nobody has a nickname.
4. **Replies about "you" keep people.** First reply ≈200 words, first and last
   sentences about the player.
5. **Romance with a mystery is what holds people longest.** Hush House keeps
   20% of its players past turn 30; Pink Tide produces the most turns of any
   world. → Slow-burn romance, a question the player wants answered (why did
   she refuse?), and a clock.
6. **Opening options are 15–25 words.** Ours were 37–43, and 94% of Second
   Skin starters tapped one, so the option *is* the first turn.

## The world in plain words

Modern city. Rain. Your apartment, fourth floor, a fire escape outside the
bedroom window. Everything is ordinary except this:

- When someone dies, a **Collector** — an angel — carries them up. Collectors
  are precise, gentle and very good at their jobs. Nobody living sees them.
- **Sera** has carried 9,999 souls without a single error. You were number
  10,000. She came for you at 2:14 a.m., looked at you for a long time, and did
  not do it. Your heart started again.
- A refusal has never happened. Heaven's answer is procedural: send the
  **Second**, a Collector who only comes when the first one fails. The Second
  for this district is **Vael**. He has never had to come twice.
- Sera's refusal cut her off. She cannot go home, her wing is torn, and she
  has **seven feathers left**.

## The one system: feathers

Sera's power is her feathers. Every miracle costs exactly one, and the player
always knows what it is being spent on:

- Hide the two of you from Heaven's sight for an hour.
- Stop time for three heartbeats.
- Heal a wound that would kill you.
- Open any door, once.
- Let the player see what she sees (the dead, the threads, other angels).

**When the seventh feather is gone, Sera stops being an angel.** She does not
know what she becomes — human, nothing, or something that falls. Neither does
the player. That uncertainty is the clock and the heart of the romance: every
time she saves you, she is spending herself.

Never shown as a number on screen. The prose says it: *"Six," she says, and
looks at her hands.* Spending one is always a beat the player sees.

## Cast (four, and only two before turn 4)

### Sera — the angel who said no
- **Looks:** young adult, around twenty in appearance, ethereal and very
  beautiful. Long, straight **silver-grey hair that shines faintly** as if lit
  from inside; pale luminous grey-blue eyes; a thin broken halo of light that
  flickers above her head when she is afraid. Simple white dress soaked dark at
  the hem; barefoot. **Two large white wings**, the left one torn near the top.
  Drawn like a seasonal anime heroine: gentle, luminous, sad.
- **Voice:** quiet, precise, formal; has never had to make small talk. Asks
  literal questions about ordinary things ("Why do you keep the light on in the
  kitchen?"). Rarely uses contractions when she is frightened.
- **Want now:** keep you alive until morning. **Long-term:** understand why she
  could not do it. **Fear:** that she refused for herself, not for you.
- **Secret:** she has been your Collector-in-waiting for six years. She watched
  you the night you nearly died before — the bridge — and you did something
  then she has never stopped thinking about. She will not say what until she
  trusts you.
- **Boundaries:** she does not lie to you. She will refuse to answer.
- **How she changes:** from duty to choice. She can fall in love, refuse to,
  leave to save you, or choose to stay and become mortal.

### Vael — the Second
- **Looks:** tall, adult, immaculate. Black suit, black gloves, **black wings
  folded close like a coat**; short ash-dark hair; calm gold eyes. Carries a
  slim ledger. Never raises his voice.
- **Voice:** courteous, dryly funny, terrifyingly reasonable. Calls the player
  by their full name. Says "I'm sorry" and means it.
- **Want now:** close the ledger — take the player, and bring Sera home before
  she is declared Fallen. **Secret:** he trained Sera. He has refused once
  himself, centuries ago, and he knows exactly what it cost.
- **Not a villain.** He can be bargained with, delayed, fooled or befriended.

### Mika — the neighbour (from turn 4 at the earliest)
- Young adult, short dyed-pink bob, oversized hoodie, night-shift nurse at the
  hospital two streets away. Comes home at 3 a.m. and is the first living person
  who can suddenly see Sera's wings — which means something has changed.
- Practical, funny, protective of the player. Knows death in a hospital way.

### Orin — the one who fell (later)
- An angel who fell three hundred years ago and runs an all-night bar under the
  train tracks. Silver earrings, burn scars where wings used to be, tired kind
  eyes. Knows what happens at seven feathers. Will tell you for a price.

## Places
Your apartment (kitchen, bedroom with fire escape) · the stairwell and roof ·
the all-night convenience store on the corner · the hospital where Mika works ·
Orin's bar under the tracks · the bridge where it happened before · the old
cathedral where Collectors gather at dawn.

## Opening (the first beat — keep it this short)

> You died at 2:14.
>
> You know because the girl standing in your kitchen tells you, very calmly, while rain drips from the ends of her silver hair onto your floor.
>
> "Four minutes ago," she says. "Your heart stopped in your sleep. I was sent to carry you up."
>
> Behind her, something enormous and white is folded against your fridge. A wing. The top of it is torn.
>
> "I didn't."
>
> Her name is Sera. She says it like it's the last thing she owns.
>
> Three slow knocks at your front door.
>
> Sera goes completely still. "That's the one they send when the first one fails. If you open it, he takes you. If you don't—" She stops.
>
> Something slides under the door. A black feather, and a card with your full name on it.

## Opening options (15–25 words, all escalate)

1. I grab Sera's hand and pull her toward the bedroom window. "Fire escape. You can explain while we run."
2. I pick up the card, step past her and open the door myself. Nobody gets hurt in my kitchen because of me.
3. I lock the door and turn to her. "You broke a rule for me. Tell me why. Fast."

## Prologue — the OOC-style character cards (four frames)

1. **The city at 2:14.** Rain on a window, a bedside clock at 2:14, a figure of
   light standing over a sleeping bed. — *"You were supposed to die tonight."* /
   *"At 2:14, in your sleep."*
2. **Sera, name card.** Portrait, wings half open, torn left wing, broken halo.
   — *"Sera"* / *"The angel sent to collect you. She refused."*
3. **Vael, name card.** Portrait in the hallway light, black wings, ledger. —
   *"Vael"* / *"The one they send when the first one fails."*
4. **Seven feathers.** Seven white feathers floating in the dark, one already
   turning to ash. — *"Seven feathers left."* / *"Every miracle costs her one."*

## Endings (eligibility, never rails; several are losses)
- **Morning.** You both live to see the sun; Sera still has feathers; Vael
  closes the ledger with a lie. (Bittersweet: she must go back.)
- **Mortal.** Sera spends her last feather on you and wakes up human. She has
  never been cold before. (Romance ending.)
- **Ash.** The last feather goes and she does not come back. (Loss.)
- **The ledger.** You open the door and go with Vael so she can go home. (Loss,
  chosen.)
- **Fallen together.** You bargain with Orin and both of you step outside
  Heaven's books for good.
- **Walk away.** You tell her to leave and mean it. She does.

## Rules for the writer
- First three beats: no new names beyond Sera and Vael; nobody orders the
  player to stay or leave; something changes every beat (a knock, a feather
  spent, the door, the window, the roof).
- Name the cost of every miracle. "Something shifts" is banned.
- The romance is earned and uncertain. Sera can say no. She can leave.
- Sera is an adult; keep everything tasteful. No graphic death.
