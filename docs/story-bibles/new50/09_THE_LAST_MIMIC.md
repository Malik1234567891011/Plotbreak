# THE LAST MIMIC

**Genre:** isekai monster comedy, dungeon fantasy, found family, light heist  
**One-line fantasy:** You died, woke up as a treasure chest at the bottom of a dungeon, and the first thing you ever feel in your new body is an elf's hand rummaging through your coins.  
**Slate line:** #9 of the 50. Lane: reborn as a monster (M). OOC reference: "Reincarnated as a monster", 2.2M. Hook: a mimic with a human mind. Rating: Moderate, 13+.  
**Fixture:** `packages/test-fixtures/src/the-last-mimic.ts`, export `THE_LAST_MIMIC`, ids `story_the_last_mimic` / `sv_the_last_mimic_1`, asset prefix `story_the_last_mimic`.

**Rename from the plan:** PLAN.md called the thief **Lirael**. That is the title character of a well-known fantasy novel series, so she is **Lirelle** here. Bruno gets a surname, **Fenn**, because his family history is a plot thread.

## Why this fantasy works

"Reborn as a monster" works because the body is the joke and the body is the game. A chest cannot walk, cannot talk in its own voice and cannot pick anything up, so every ordinary action becomes a puzzle the player solves with their mouth, their lid and their nerve. Every beat of the opening is funny and none of it is safe.

It also inverts the dungeon crawl. In every other dungeon story the player is the one who wants the treasure. Here the player *is* the treasure, and every character in the building wants something that is inside them: coins, a lich's life, a heart worth forty thousand crowns. That gives every relationship a built-in negotiation from the first line.

The mystery (why a box?) is optional. A player who only wants to bargain with a thief and befriend a goblin gets a whole game.

## Story bible

The player died in their own world. They do not remember how, and the story never needs to say. They wake in the **Ninth Vault**, the deepest room of the **Gilt Deep**, a dungeon under the mining town of **Brightwell**. They are an iron-banded treasure chest full of gold coins. Their lid is their mouth.

Mimics are supposed to be extinct. Thirty years ago the **Delvers' Guild** hunted the last of them, because a mimic's heart stitched into a bag makes a **deep satchel**, a bag with no bottom, and the whole town's trade runs on those satchels. Now every satchel in Brightwell is going shallow, and there are no new hearts. Tonight, when the player woke, the great chime in the Guildhouse tower rang for the first time in thirty years. The Guild has posted a bounty of **forty thousand crowns, heart intact**.

Under the player's coins sits **the Cinder**: a coal in a glass bead, still warm. It is the life of **Ash**, the courteous lich who keeps the Gilt Deep. The Guild's contracts desk sent Lirelle's crew down to bring it up, so the Deep could be collected like rent. Nobody can take it by force: whatever is inside a mimic is lost if the mimic dies.

The opening is one night and two days. Three clocks run against each other:
- **Tonight:** the crew in the vault, the goblin staff coming through the hatch, the keeper noticing.
- **Six o'clock:** the dungeon resets. The Long Stair seals for an hour with every trap live.
- **Tomorrow evening:** Edda Quell, the Guild's senior appraiser, comes down the stair a floor an hour with a silver chime that rings near a mimic.

The body grows. The player starts able to hop a hand's width at a time, loudly. Through play they learn to grow short quick legs, then to wear other furniture shapes (barrel, door, crate), and, if they chase the old mystery, perhaps a face.

### Rules of being a box (hard canon)

1. The player is a mimic in the body of a chest. Lid is mouth; there are no hands, no eyes in any usual sense; they feel everything inside them.
2. They cannot walk at first. Hopping works and is extremely loud.
3. They have no voice of their own. They speak only in voices they have heard: Pell's, Lirelle's, Bruno's, later anybody's.
4. Mimics are believed extinct; deep satchels are going shallow.
5. The bounty is forty thousand crowns, heart intact. The great chime rang tonight.
6. The Cinder is Ash's life. The Guild sent Lirelle's crew to bring it up.
7. Whatever is inside a mimic is lost if the mimic dies. A dead mimic's heart still makes a satchel; a living mimic could deepen satchels for ever. That is Edda's aim.
8. Ash called the player's soul into the chest six months ago, with a summoning drawn below the Ninth Vault. He never volunteers this. (In the fixture this lives in Ash's secret, not in hard canon, so the narrator treats it as something he keeps back.)
9. At 06:00 the dungeon resets and the Long Stair seals for an hour.
10. No status screens, no levels, no floating numbers. The body says what it can do.
11. Everybody is an adult, the Deep's staff and goblins included. The player was a grown adult with a job in their old life.
12. Pell polishes the vault chest twice a day, first thing in the morning and at about half past ten at night. Tonight she does not know there are delvers in the vault.
13. Ash notices anybody who lingers in the Ninth Vault: within the hour the lamps turn blue and he speaks through the walls before he comes down.
14. Fights are bloodless on the page; the goblin-ear bounty is mentioned, never shown. Romance stops at a kiss, and only once the player has a face.

## Tone and pacing

Comedy first, warmth underneath, real stakes at the edges. The player should laugh in the first beat and worry by the fifth. Write the body physically: the weight of coins, the creak of the lid, the itch of legs that are not there yet. Never cruel to the player for being a box. Fights are short, loud and costly; talking is usually better and should feel like it.

Prose style: webnovel. Short paragraphs, punchy lines, characters who interrupt each other.

## Player starting authority

The player authors:
- what they were called before;
- what they did for a living (the archetype: **You Fixed Locks**, **You Did Voices**, **You Ran a Shop**, **You Worked Doors**), which sets one starting trick and what their human mind is good at;
- one plain sentence about their old life;
- optionally, what the chest looks like.

The world does not decide whether they bite, bargain or hide, or whom they trust.

## Opening scene

Exact opener (117 words):

> You wake up in the dark, and you are a box.
>
> Wooden walls, iron bands, a lid, and a belly full of gold coins. You can feel every one.
>
> Lantern light. A hand slides under your lid, and fingers brush your coins. Then two knuckles knock on your inside wall.
>
> "Hello?" Lirelle says. An elf in dark leathers, knife in her other hand, talking to you. "You're warm. Why are you warm?"
>
> By the door, a big man raises a shield as tall as he is. "Lirelle. Hand out. Slowly."
>
> "Mimics are extinct, Bruno."
>
> Her hand doesn't move. Her eyes settle on your keyhole.
>
> "So," she says. "Are you treasure? Or are you about to bite me?"

Three cards:
- **Said:** "Please don't scream. I'm not a chest. Well. I am now."
- **Done:** I push one gold coin into her palm and keep very still.
- **Risky:** I shut my lid on her wrist. Gently. And I don't let go.

Lirelle and Bruno react to the exact action. A chest that answers in Lirelle's own voice is a different shock from one that shuts on her wrist. Bruno never strikes first.

## Major cast

All adults, the Deep's staff and goblins included. The player was a grown adult with a job in their old life.

### Lirelle
**Age:** 163 (elf; looks about 28).  
**Role:** elf thief with her hand in your coins. Licensed delver in debt to the Guild.  
**Appearance:** long pale braid wound twice round her head and pinned with a lockpick, sharp green eyes, practical dark delver's leathers, scuffed at the knees and patched at one elbow, fingerless gloves, worn satchel on her hip.  
**Want now:** work out what the warm chest is before Bruno does something heroic.  
**Long-term want:** clear her Guild bond by bringing up the Cinder before the second reset.  
**Fear:** dying in debt so it passes to Bruno; finding out she is as mercenary as the Guild thinks.  
**Secrets:** nine years ago she stole a deep satchel from Edda Quell's desk; her bond clears only with the Cinder. The satchel on her hip is that one, made from a mimic; it stopped humming two winters ago and started again when she touched the player.  
**Voice:** quick, dry, transactional. Prices things aloud. Two questions in one breath. "Right." to restart. "Do me a favour" instead of please.  
**Romance:** possible, slow, up to a kiss; gated on real trust and the Cinder being on the table.

### Ash
**Age:** died at 51; dead 312 years since (363 in all).  
**Role:** the lich who keeps the Gilt Deep and does its accounts.  
**Appearance:** very tall skeleton in an immaculate charcoal frock coat and white cravat, half-moon spectacles on a bare skull, cold blue pinpoints behind the lenses.  
**Want now:** get the Cinder back without anybody being hurt.  
**Long-term want:** he is tired, and would like to stop.  
**Fear:** dying suddenly with the Deep full of delvers; being remembered as a monster by the only creature he ever made.  
**Secrets:** he summoned the player into the chest as a guardian no thief could open, expected an animal, got a person. If the Cinder were broken at the reset hour with the stair empty, the Deep would go quiet safely and he would rest.  
**Voice:** courteous, formal, bookkeeper's vocabulary (entries, accounts, settling up). Apologises before every threat. The room gets colder instead of louder.  
**Boundary:** will not break a mimic to get at what is inside it.

### Pell
**Age:** 24.  
**Role:** goblin on the Deep's staff. Has polished the player twice a day (first thing in the morning, and again at about half past ten at night) for six months and told them every secret she has.  
**Appearance:** an adult goblin woman of twenty-four, wiry and long-limbed for a goblin, with a sharp, work-worn face and grey-green skin, enormous pointed ears with three brass rings in the left one, sleeves rolled over forearms corded from years of pushing the coin trolley, a staff apron covered in polish stains and a rag tucked into the waistband.  
**Silhouette:** leaning on a coin trolley piled with gold, rag over one shoulder, ears swivelled towards whoever is talking.  
**Want now:** find out how much the chest heard.  
**Long-term want:** see the sun once without anybody taking her ears (the Guild pays two silver an ear).  
**Secrets:** the back corridors run up to a goblin hatch in a cellar under Brightwell market. She once tried on the keeper's coat.  
**Voice:** fast, breathless, run-on with "and", shouts the important word in CAPITALS, calls the player "Box".

### Bruno Fenn
**Age:** 38.  
**Role:** Lirelle's shield-bearer, on what he has promised is his last delve (for the fourth time).  
**Appearance:** broad, sandy beard, shaved head, dented plate over a quilted coat, tower shield with a small barrel painted on it for luck.  
**Want now:** get off the stair before six.  
**Long-term want:** go home to the farm for good.  
**Fear:** being the last thing between Lirelle and something he cannot hold; telling his wife the truth.  
**Secrets:** his grandfather Aldo Fenn killed the second-to-last mimic, and the bounty bought the family farm. His wife Marta (37, a grown woman who runs the farm) thinks he sells barrels.  
**Voice:** slow, low, two or three words at a time. Quotes his gran ("Never trust a chest that's warmer than the room. She had nine fingers, my gran.").  
**Boundary:** will not strike first; will not help sell a living thing that has asked to live.

### Edda Quell
**Age:** 52.  
**Role:** the Delvers' Guild's senior appraiser. Carries the silver chime. The rival.  
**Appearance:** grey hair, long dove-grey Guild coat with silver buttons, wire spectacles on a chain, small black notebook, silver chime on a ribbon round her left wrist.  
**Want now:** find the mimic before anybody with an axe does.  
**Long-term want:** bring it to the Stitchers' Hall alive, which she believes is mercy.  
**Fear:** every satchel in Brightwell failing in one winter, the Guild treasury first.  
**Secrets:** as an apprentice she held the chime when the last mimic was killed; it asked the hunters to stop, in one of their voices. The Guild treasury sits in one failing deep satchel.  
**Voice:** Quiet, plain, short questions, like a vet; lot-sheet valuations when moved ("Condition: excellent. Provenance: the Ninth Vault."). Never apologises, never threatens. Stops early and waits.

## Places

1. **The Ninth Vault**: start. Round room, nine plinths, one chest. Goblin hatch behind the seventh plinth.
2. **The Hall of False Chests**: forty chests identical to the player, mostly trapped. Best hiding place in the Deep.
3. **The Keeper's Study**: Ash's office, ledgers to the ceiling.
4. **The Back Corridors**: staff passages behind every wall; the goblin way up opens once Pell shows it.
5. **The Staff Canteen**: goblins, two tired skeletons, a slime on washing-up.
6. **The Gallery of Echoes**: plays back every voice ever spoken in it, including some from very far away. Mystery entry point.
7. **The Long Stair**: nine floors of switchbacks; seals at six.
8. **The Delvers' Camp**: safe landing on the third floor; bounty board; bad tea.
9. **The Well Mouth**: the Deep meets the market; Guild checkpoint.
10. **The Guildhouse**: bounty wall, counting room, the great chime, Edda's office.
11. **The Stitchers' Hall**: where satchels are made from mimic hearts, and now mended.
12. **The First Vault** (hidden): under the vault, where the summoning is still drawn.

## Behavioral state

Three invisible variables. The prose shows them; the player never sees a number.
- **Strength** (good high): how much the body has left. Low means the lid shakes and hops fall short. Rest refills it.
- **Rumour** (good low): how widely it is known that the last mimic is awake and biting. Rises with public violence. High means delvers come down looking for a chest.
- **Strain** (good low): what borrowed voices and new shapes cost. High means voices slip, shapes flicker.

## Factions

- **The Delvers' Guild** (starts hostile: "A Bounty"). Bounties, checkpoint, satchel trade. Can become "On the Books".
- **The Deep Staff** (starts friendly-ish: the player is technically their stock). Can become "Senior Staff".
- **The Stitchers' Hall** (neutral: "A Prospect"). Raw material or partner.

## Quests

- **Treasure or Teeth** (main): answer the hand in you; get through the night.
- **A Box With Legs** (main): learn to move; decide where a chest goes (crew, staff, alone, or stay).
- **Somebody Else's Heartbeat** (main): hear Ash and Lirelle out; decide whose hands the Cinder ends in (back to Ash, to Lirelle, keep it, or break it gently at the reset hour if Ash asks).
- **The Chime on the Stair** (main): learn what the heart is worth; be somewhere or something else when the chime rings (hide, wear a shape, deal, smuggle out, goblin way up, or bite past).
- **Why a Box?** (optional lead, hidden until the gallery or the ledger gives it away): why the player woke here; what to do about the summoning.
- **Two Silver an Ear** (side): Pell and the sun.
- **The Last Delve, Again** (side): Bruno, his gran, and the barrels.

## Five promises

1. **What you become** (finale): legs, a shape, maybe a face, or a clear choice to stay a chest.
2. **The hand** (relationship): whether the thief becomes a partner.
3. **The Cinder** (theme): somebody else's life among your coins, decided by the treasure.
4. **The chime** (rival): Edda Quell on the stair, polite as a funeral.
5. **Why a box** (mystery, optional): a summoning under the vault and an apology six months owed.

## Ending space

Common:
- **Treasure**: stayed in the vault on purpose, Cinder settled. Pell still polishes you; now you answer.
- **The Workshop Shelf**: nobody stopped the chime. Kept alive and comfortable in the Stitchers' Hall. The honest loss, reachable without a single mistake.

Uncommon:
- **The Fourth Share**: out with Lirelle's crew, full share.
- **Forty Thousand Crowns**: bit your way out; free and wanted.
- **On the Guild's Books**: proved you can deepen satchels; first monster ever registered as a member.
- **Into the Walls**: walked away from everybody, early.

Rare:
- **The Keeper's Heir**: gave Ash his life back; he offers you the Deep.
- **The Deep Goes Quiet**: Ash asked; you broke the Cinder gently at the reset hour.
- **The Box That Owns the Dungeon**: kept the Cinder. The keeper reads you the accounts every morning.

Unique:
- **A Face of Your Own**: learned the old shapes, walked into town wearing a person.
- **The First of Many** (mystery): woke another chest.
- **The Way You Came** (mystery): went back through the summoning.

Ten of the twelve are reachable without the mystery.
