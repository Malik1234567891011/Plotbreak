## Why this fantasy works

Forced proximity is the oldest engine in the romance shelf, and it works because it removes the
question "will they meet again" and replaces it with "what happens at two in the morning when they
both want the kettle". This version gives the proximity a clock and a referee: the housing office
cannot fix it until an appeals hearing on Friday, so for six days two strangers share one unit, one
bathroom and one door that locks.

The fame is the second engine. Yuri Sakamoto has two voices, the bright one two million people hear
every night and the flat, tired, funny one you get through the bedroom door. The player is the only
person on campus who hears both. That is the romance: not that she is famous, but that the player
meets the person underneath before anybody else does.

The third engine is a secret with stakes. She needs this building because her old address leaked.
A campus gossip account is offering money for her door. The player can protect that, ignore it,
or sell it, and the story has a real ending for each.

Like Good Morning, Husband, this story has to be good when nothing "important" happens. Thirty turns
of arguing about the bathroom schedule, cooking noodles at two in the morning and helping her pass a
statistics course is a complete way to play it.

## Story bible

The player is a third-year undergraduate at **Westmere University**. For two years they commuted
ninety minutes each way. This year they finally won the housing lottery: **Room 12C in Linden
House**, the newest residence on campus, twelve floors up with a window over the river.

12C is a single-occupancy unit: one bedroom with the only lockable door, a living room with a
fold-out sofa, a kitchenette with two hobs, and one bathroom with one shelf.

On move-in Saturday, at twenty to eleven, **Yuri Sakamoto** arrives with four pink suitcases, an iced
coffee the size of a vase and a printed assignment email for the same room. She streams as
*yurisaka* to about two million followers. Half the campus has her face as a phone wallpaper.

The housing office has four hundred move-in tickets and no empty bed anywhere. **Ms. Park**, who runs
it, rules that both of them are lawfully resident in 12C until the appeals hearing on **Friday at
ten**. One of them gets the bedroom. The other gets the sofa.

What the player does not know:

- **Why she is here.** Three weeks ago her old downtown address leaked. People have waited outside
  it with cameras since. She needs key-card gates and a staffed front desk, and she has told her
  chat she is travelling.
- **How she got the room.** Her assignment has no lottery number, only a reference code beginning
  `OVR`. Her manager, **Haneul Seo**, asked the university's communications office for a secure
  room. Someone above Ms. Park entered an override with a dean's code and never cancelled the
  player's assignment. Yuri does not know. Ms. Park found the code on move-in morning.
- **Who is hunting her.** **Quadwatch**, an anonymous campus gossip account with forty thousand
  followers, posts a bounty on Sunday morning: five hundred dollars for her building, a thousand for
  her door. It is run by **Jonah Whitlock**, the friendly neighbour in 12D who is always filming
  "for a project".
- **What Ben is.** The player's best friend, **Ben Castillo**, has been a volunteer moderator in her
  chat for eighteen months under the name *lanternfish*. He deleted the address leak at three in the
  morning. She has never seen his face.
- **What she is failing.** A required statistics course. One more failed course is academic
  probation, and her parents think streaming is a hobby.

Nothing supernatural happens. Nobody has powers. A shove is a shove: violence brings campus
security, a conduct referral and a voided housing contract.

### Playable span

Six days, Saturday move-in to the Friday hearing, on one campus and one downtown street. The week
after the hearing lives in the endings.

## Tone and pacing

- Fast, funny, specific. Webnovel cadence: short beats, a face at the top of the beat.
- The comedy is domestic: the bathroom shelf, the 5:40 alarm, the trombone in 12F, whose turn it is
  to buy milk, the sofa bar in your back.
- The tension is privacy. Phones are everywhere. The lobby is the most dangerous room in the book.
- Two voices for Yuri. The stream voice is bright and an octave up. The real one is flat, precise and
  very dry. When she slips into sincerity she says "Don't" and changes the subject.
- Romance is slow, earned and optional. Flirting, tension and one kiss at most, on the page.
- **13+.** No fan service. Separate rooms, a locked door, a bathroom schedule. No lingering on bodies,
  no sexual content, nothing about anyone's age except that they are adults.
- Yuri can refuse, sulk, win, lose, and move out. She is never a reward.

## Player starting authority

The player is a BLANK protagonist: they type their own name and pronouns. They are a third-year
undergraduate, twenty or older. The archetype is what they study or do, which is the reason they
needed this room. Everything else, including whether they keep the room, tell anyone, go on stream
or fall for her, is decided in play.

### Minimal hard canon

- Everyone is an adult (ages below).
- Both of them hold a valid assignment for 12C until Friday at ten.
- Yuri needs a secure building because her address leaked, and has told nobody at the university.
- The override came from above Ms. Park, at Haneul's request. Yuri does not know.
- Jonah runs Quadwatch. Ben is *lanternfish*.
- No powers, no supernatural. Romance goes no further than a kiss.

### Yuri Sakamoto (21)

**Role:** your roommate, the most famous person on campus.
**Wants now:** the bedroom, a locked door, and nobody knowing which door it is.
**Wants underneath:** to finish her degree as a person rather than a channel; to be liked by
somebody who has never seen the stream.
**Fears:** the leak happening twice; that without the voice she is boring.
**Secrets:** why she needs the building; the statistics course; she cries after bad streams and
checks the lock three times.
**Voice:** flat, precise, deadpan, terms-of-service phrasing ("Rule four:"). The stream voice is
bright and an octave up, and she hates it being mentioned. Sincerity, then "Don't."
**Visual hook:** a giant iced coffee she is never without, sunglasses pushed up into black hair with
a pink streak.

### Ben Castillo (20)

**Role:** your best friend since first-year orientation. Lives in Hargrove Hall, the oldest dorm,
and has a floor you can sleep on.
**Wants:** you to be happy, and never ever to be found out as *lanternfish*.
**Fears:** being the fan who made it weird; losing you to a bigger life.
**Secret:** he moderates her chat and deleted the leak. He knows the downtown street by heart.
**Voice:** fast, loud, a running commentary, three "okay"s before a sentence, then a quiet true line.
**Visual hook:** a vintage film camera on a strap and a beanie in every weather.

### Haneul Seo (29)

**Role:** Yuri's manager. Lives two floors below Yuri's old flat downtown.
**Wants:** to keep Yuri famous and safe this week, and a sponsorship renewal that needs numbers.
**Fears:** being the reason the leak happened; being right about content and wrong about Yuri.
**Secret:** she arranged the override through the comms office and has not told Yuri.
**Voice:** bright, professional, calendar-speak. Everything is "a moment". Polite steel.
**Visual hook:** two phones, one in each hand, both ringing.

### Kit Navarro (22, they/them)

**Role:** resident assistant for floor twelve. Free room in exchange for enforcing every rule.
**Wants:** a quiet floor and to finish their nursing placement.
**Fears:** losing the RA job, which is their housing.
**Secret:** they are one write-up away from losing the post themselves.
**Voice:** weary, clinical, rules recited with their whole heart; shift-worker bluntness.
**Visual hook:** a lanyard heavy with master keys over nursing scrubs.

### Jonah Whitlock (21)

**Role:** the neighbour in 12D. Charming, always filming, everybody's friend.
**Wants:** the thousand dollars; tuition is due in nine days.
**Fears:** being unmasked; being the bad guy in somebody else's story.
**Secret:** he runs Quadwatch.
**Voice:** smooth, complimentary, "hypothetically", talks about timing and clicks.
**Visual hook:** a small gimbal camera always in one hand.

### Ms. Park Eun-ha (48)

**Role:** housing coordinator for nineteen years. Chairs Friday's hearing.
**Wants:** her lottery back, clean, and four hundred tickets closed.
**Fears:** that the system she built is now for sale.
**Secret:** she found the override code on Saturday morning and it came from above her.
**Voice:** dry, tired, procedural; calls people by ticket number until she likes them.
**Visual hook:** reading glasses pushed up into grey-streaked hair, a printer she hits to make it work.

## Five promises

1. **FINALE, who sleeps in 12C on Friday night.** Seed: two printed emails with the same room.
   Payoff: the hearing in Room 204.
2. **RELATIONSHIP, the person she is when the camera is off.** Seed: a voice that drops an octave the
   second the stream ends. Payoff: the roof, or noodles at two in the morning, and what she says there.
3. **MYSTERY, why the system gave her your room.** Seed: her email has no lottery number, only a code
   beginning OVR. Payoff: an override signed above Ms. Park's head, requested by Haneul.
4. **RIVAL, whoever runs Quadwatch.** Seed: a bounty for her door, and a neighbour who is always
   filming. Payoff: Jonah, and the tuition bill he needs the money for.
5. **THEME, a door that locks.** Seed: she checks the lock three times. Payoff: the leak, the street
   downtown, and whether anyone keeps her door a secret.

## The opener

One hundred and two words, under the cap of one hundred and twenty. Yuri speaks inside the first sixty. It ends on a demand
that has to be answered now. No proper noun the player needs to know already.

> Four pink suitcases thump into your doorway.
>
> The woman behind them pulls down a face mask. Hoodie three sizes too big, sunglasses indoors, an
> iced coffee the size of a vase. Half the campus has her face as a phone wallpaper.
>
> "Yuri Sakamoto. No, I’m not live. Yes, it’s really me."
>
> She holds up a printed email: Room 12C. Her name.
>
> You hold up yours. Room 12C. Your name.
>
> Her phone buzzes. She silences it without looking.
>
> "Housing says this is my room." She takes a long, slow sip. "You have until I finish this coffee
> to prove it’s yours. Starting now."

Suggestions, three different moves:

- **Said:** "You could live anywhere. Why do you want a dorm room this badly?"
- **Done:** I sit down on the bare mattress and start unpacking, very slowly, while she watches.
- **Risky:** I take the coffee out of her hand and finish it in one long gulp.
