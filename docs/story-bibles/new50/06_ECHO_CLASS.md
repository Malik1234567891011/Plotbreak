# ECHO CLASS

**Slate:** #6 of the 50-story plan (`docs/stories-50/PLAN.md` §3). Drop 6.
**Genre:** superpower academy, rivalry, action, light romance, comedy
**Audience lean:** male (OOC evidence: Hero Academia 21.6M, Relic Academy 4.2M)
**One-line fantasy:** You copy any power you touch for sixty seconds. You just shook hands with the strongest student in the building, and now you are floating.
**Fixture:** `packages/test-fixtures/src/echo-class.ts` (`ECHO_CLASS`, storyId `story_echo_class`)

## Why this fantasy works

The copy power is the most flexible power fantasy there is: every person in the academy is a possible weapon, and the only price is a handshake. That makes every character the player meets a decision, not scenery. Who do you touch before a fight? Whose hand do you refuse?

Sixty seconds keeps it honest. The player is strong in bursts and powerless in between, so every fight has a clock, every borrowed power has to be learned in ten seconds, and the prose can count down. Copying is not free for the other person either: while you hold their power, they share it with you. That one rule turns a handshake into a kindness, a theft, or a sabotage, depending on whose hand it is and when.

The famous moment is turn one. The player does not wait to find out what they can do. They are already in the air, in front of everybody, holding the top-ranked student's power and her hand, and she wants both back.

## Story bible

**Larkspur Academy** trains licensed heroes. It is a four-year, post-secondary academy on a hill above **Velling**, a harbour city. It admits at eighteen. Every student and every member of staff in this story is an adult.

Twelve hundred students are ranked on **the Board**, a wall-sized display in the Great Hall that updates at seven every morning. Rank moves only through sanctioned challenges in **the Pit**, refereed by faculty: anyone may challenge anyone. Win and you take their place; lose to somebody far above you and you drop fifty. The top ten get the first placements with the rescue services and the first licences. Off-the-books fights happen in **the Boathouse** and count for nothing except reputation.

About one adult in forty has a power. The national **Registry** classifies every power into a class: Field (changes an area), Form (changes your own body), Sense (perceives what others cannot), Make (produces something), and **Echo**.

Echo class has had exactly one registrant before the player: **Arlo Wren**, twenty-six years ago, when he was twenty-four (he is fifty now). What happened to him is not public.

The player transfers into the second year. Their power:

- Skin-to-skin contact with someone who has a power gives the player a copy of it for **sixty seconds**. They feel the count under their ribs.
- One copy at a time. A new touch replaces the old one.
- Fabric stops it. Gloves, sleeves, a jacket: nothing copies.
- While the player holds a copy, the original owner keeps their power but **shares control of it**. It answers them half a beat late and feels like somebody else has a hand on it. When the copy ends, it is theirs alone again.
- When a copy ends, there is a drop: a headache, heavy limbs, and for a minute the player can still feel the shape of what they lost.
- Without a copy, the player has no power at all. Unranked transfers are placed last, so the player starts at **1,200**.

By tradition, the top-ranked student shakes every transfer's hand at the welcome assembly. This year that is **Yuna Takeda**, ranked first for fourteen months, whose power is gravity. The story opens twenty seconds into that handshake.

**The hidden rule.** Sixty seconds is a habit, not a law. A copy can be held past it by refusing to let it end. Every minute past sixty, more of the power leaves its owner and settles in the player. Let go within a few minutes and it goes home, bruised. Hold it for ten and it does not go home at all. Arlo Wren held a copy for eleven minutes, holding up a collapsing pier over the harbour while sixty-one people got off it. He saved all of them. The power he held belonged to his classmate, **Imogen Adair**, and she has not had it since. The Registry restricted Echo class the same week.

The player cannot do this, and does not know it is possible, until somebody tells them or they find the file. The ability is granted by the quest step where they learn it and gated by the flag that learning sets.

## Tone and pacing

- Shonen academy energy with a real cost underneath. Fast, bright, loud, funny, and then suddenly not.
- Fights are legible: who touched whom, what power is in your chest, how many seconds are left. When a copy is running, the prose counts.
- Rank numbers everywhere. People are called by their rank before their name, and it is rude.
- Comedy comes from Teo and from the absurdity of borrowed powers used badly (copying a plant-grower in a fistfight).
- Romance is slow, teasing and earned. Yuna can say no, and does. Up to a kiss, never more, and never fan service.
- Nobody is a monster. Rook is desperate, not evil. The Registry has a real reason. Adair has the best reason of all.
- 13+: fights bruise, people get knocked down and winded, nobody bleeds out on the page.

## Player starting authority

The protagonist is **BLANK**: the player names themselves and sets pronouns. They are a second-year transfer, twenty years old. The archetype question sets where they learned to use the copy before Larkspur:

- **Stunt Double:** two years of film stunts on borrowed powers. Fast, showy, good at getting a hand on people.
- **Street Fighter:** harbour-front fight clubs where nobody knew what you could do. Hard to knock down with no power at all.
- **Clinic Orderly:** night shifts at a clinic for people whose powers were hurting them. Reads a power fast, calms people down.
- **Never Used It:** wore gloves every day and hid it your whole life. Iron self-control and unknown limits.

### Minimal hard canon

- Everybody at Larkspur is an adult; it admits at eighteen.
- Copy rules as above: skin contact, sixty seconds, one at a time, fabric stops it, the owner shares control while you hold it.
- Yuna Takeda is ranked first and her power is gravity.
- Rank only moves through sanctioned challenges in the Pit.
- One Echo before the player: Arlo Wren, twenty-six years ago, a twenty-four-year-old Larkspur fourth-year then and fifty now.
- The player is twenty. Touching somebody with no power copies nothing; touching Adair, the player feels an empty place, as if something used to be there.
- The Registrar must classify the player by **Friday at ten**: open (stay and compete) or restricted (leave with the Registry).
- Holding past sixty is real, costly, and unknown to the player at the start.

## Opening scene (the opener)

> Your feet leave the floor.
>
> The student whose hand you just shook does not let go. First of twelve hundred: the hall told you that with its applause. Now she is staring at you like you picked her pocket.
>
> "Give it back," Yuna says.
>
> Chairs scrape behind you. A water jug lifts off the welcome table and turns slowly in the air. Two hundred faces tilt up to follow you.
>
> Her power sits under your ribs, heavy as wet sand, and something in there is counting down.
>
> Forty seconds left.
>
> Her grip tightens. "How are you doing that? Give it back. Now."

102 words. Yuna speaks at word 40. It ends on a demand. The only proper noun is Yuna, introduced in the beat.

Suggestions, one of each kind:

- **Said:** "I'm trying! I've never held anything this heavy. How do you switch it off?"
- **Done:** I let go of her hand and try to sink back down to the floor.
- **Risky:** I tighten my grip and pull us both up toward the ceiling.

Every route through the handshake is real: hand it back, use it, talk her down, feel out what you are holding (which is how the player first notices how much Yuna is carrying), or let go of her and grab somebody else. At sixty seconds the copy ends and the player drops. Professor Adair, on the stage, is the first adult to reach them. She asks three questions and checks her watch.

## Major cast

Ages are canon. Everybody is 18 or older.

### Yuna Takeda (21)
**Role:** second-year, ranked #1 of 1,200 for fourteen months. Gravity (Field class): she can make anything she can see within about thirty metres up to ten times heavier, or nearly weightless, herself included.
**Appearance:** black hair cut bluntly at the jaw, grey eyes, academy jacket zipped to the collar. **Visual hook:** two steel wrist weights, four kilos each, which she never takes off.
**Want:** keep #1 to graduation and get a licence and a place with the sea-rescue service. She grew up in a port town and wants rescue work, not fame.
**Fear:** losing control in front of people. The day her power gets bigger than she is.
**Secrets:** her gravity has been getting stronger for a year. She sleeps with her bed bolted to the floor and the weights on, because twice she has woken up against the ceiling. And when the player copied her, the weight was shared for the first time in her life, and for a minute she could breathe all the way in. "Give it back" was fear. What came after was relief, and that frightens her more.
**Voice:** quiet, exact, measures everything in kilos and seconds. Dry humour that arrives a beat late. "You weigh seventy-one kilos. I know, because for a minute I was carrying it."
**Arc options:** rival, reluctant partner, the person on the roof at midnight, a kiss after a match she won, a title she lost because of you.

### Teo Vance (22)
**Role:** your roommate in Room 4C. Second-year, ranked #388. Hears electricity (Sense class): wiring in walls, phones in pockets, the hum of the Board. Support track, Adair's lab assistant, and the dorm's supplier of instant noodles and odds.
**Appearance:** tall and loose-limbed, curly brown hair, a canvas jacket with too many pockets. **Visual hook:** over-ear headphones that are never plugged into anything.
**Want:** stay above #400 by the end of term, or his scholarship goes to review.
**Secret:** Rook pays him to report on every transfer. He owes Rook money from a bad book (he bet against Yuna). He has been told to report on you by tonight, and he likes you, which is the problem.
**Voice:** fast, warm, everything in odds, ends sentences with "yeah?" "I had you at four to one to survive the week. Moved you to three. That's a compliment, yeah?"

### Professor Imogen Adair (47)
**Role:** head of Power Theory and the combat fundamentals instructor. Was going to be one of the best in the country; the faculty think an injury ended it in her fourth year, before she was licensed.
**Appearance:** cropped dark hair with a grey streak, rolled shirtsleeves, a long scar down her right forearm. **Visual hook:** a wristwatch worn on the inside of her wrist, which she checks every sixty seconds without seeming to know she does it.
**Want:** to find out whether you can hold past sixty before the Registry does, and if you can, to teach you why you must not.
**Hidden drive:** part of her wants to know whether her power still exists anywhere.
**Secrets:** twenty-six years ago, as a fourth-year (she was twenty-one), her power (she could stop any moving object she looked at, for as long as she kept looking) was copied by her classmate Arlo Wren during the Velling pier collapse. He held it eleven minutes. It never came back. And she called the Registry herself the morning your transfer file arrived.
**Voice:** a lecturer's precision and a drill instructor's economy. Asks questions in threes. Says "Again." as a full sentence. Talks about her own career in the past tense and never says why.

### Callum Rook, called Rook (23)
**Role:** third-year, ranked #9, head of the Back Row: twenty-odd students who sit at the back of every room and settle things in the Boathouse. Recoil (Form class): any force that hits him, he can store and return doubled. He wins by letting people hit him first.
**Appearance:** big through the shoulders, pale blond hair slicked back, jacket open over a black shirt, knuckles that have never been split. **Visual hook:** a black chess rook he walks across his knuckles and gives to people he has decided are his.
**Want:** #1 before he graduates. He challenges Yuna on Thursday at four. He wants the player to shake her hand in the Pit the second before the bell: sixty seconds of shared control is all he needs.
**Hidden drive:** his mother and grandfather were both #1 at Larkspur. He is the first in his family who is not. He decided cruelty was efficient.
**Boundaries:** never throws the first punch. Never lies about the terms of a deal.
**Secrets:** he has challenged Yuna four times off the books and lost all four. And his grandfather sat on the Registry board that restricted Arlo Wren, so Rook knows what Echo class can do past sixty seconds.
**Voice:** lazy, polite, smiling. Calls everybody by their rank number. Never swears. Offers deals like a shopkeeper. "Morning, Twelve-Hundred. Hit me. Everybody does eventually, and it's the best part of my day."

### Registrar Delphine Marsh, called Marsh (52)
**Role:** classification officer for the national Registry. Has set up in the Registry Annex with one file in her case, and it is yours. She has never had a power; shaking her hand copies nothing, and she offers it anyway.
**Appearance:** neat grey bob, navy suit that has been to a lot of hearings, clipboard with a carbon under every sheet. **Visual hook:** a fountain pen she uncaps only when she is about to write something you cannot take back.
**Want:** classify you by Friday at ten. She does not want to restrict a twenty-year-old. She has done it once.
**Secrets:** standing order: any Echo who demonstrably holds past sixty is restricted on the spot, with no hearing, and there is a car waiting at the harbour. And she processed Arlo Wren's restriction as a junior clerk. He is alive, in a Registry facility, and every year she visits he asks about Adair.
**Voice:** gentle, procedural, reads her own questions aloud and writes down the pauses. "For the record, that was a yes. You can change it. I'll write down that you changed it."

### Arlo Wren (50)
Off the page until the Visiting Hours ending. Twenty-six years ago he was a 24-year-old Larkspur fourth-year and Adair's classmate when he held her power for eleven minutes at the pier collapse; he is now held in a Registry facility four hours down the coast, and asks Marsh about Adair every year.

## Behavioural state (invisible)

- **Stamina** (restorable): the body's budget for copies, fights and long conversations. Rest and noodles bring it back.
- **Scrutiny** (bad when high): how hard the faculty and the Registry are looking at you. Public fights and grabbing people raise it. It decides how Friday goes.
- **Overlap** (bad when high): how much of other people's powers is sticking to you. Low is clean. High, copies stop ending cleanly, you catch other people's habits, and the hold is right there.

- **The Quad:** the hub. Steps, a fountain, everyone eating lunch where they can be seen.
- **Residence Block:** east and west wings, a shared kitchen, a noticeboard of challenge results.

- **Adair's Lab:** Power Theory. Measuring rigs, a slow-motion camera, a wall clock with a loud second hand.
- **Records Room 0:** the locked basement under the lab where the sealed Registry files are. Opens only once Teo has heard its lock.
- **The Infirmary:** for drops, bruises and people who copied the wrong power.
- **The Registry Annex:** a borrowed office with a borrowed desk and Marsh's carbon copies.
- **The Boathouse:** the Back Row's place on the shore. Off-the-books fights, a bowl of black rooks.
- **Velling Harbour:** the town below. Noodle shop, ferry, the rebuilt pier with a bronze plaque listing the sixty-one people who walked off it.

## Five promises

1. **RIVAL: What Rook wants from one handshake.** Seed: a black rook left on your pillow. Payoff: Thursday at four, the Pit, and whose hand you take before the bell.
2. **RELATIONSHIP: What the top-ranked student is carrying.** Seed: four-kilo weights she never takes off. Payoff: the roof at midnight, and the only person who can take half of it for sixty seconds.
3. **MYSTERY: The only other Echo.** Seed: a professor who checks her watch every sixty seconds. Payoff: a sealed file, a pier, eleven minutes, and the power Adair has not had since.
4. **THEME: Sixty seconds is a habit, not a limit.** Seed: the count under your ribs always stops at zero, as if something were letting go. Payoff: the hold, and what it costs the person whose power it is.
5. **FINALE: Open or restricted, by Friday at ten.** Seed: "Class: pending", in pencil, underlined twice. Payoff: the hearing, and who stands up in it.
