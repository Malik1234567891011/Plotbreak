# 13 — Three Heroines and a Porter

**Card line:** They got SSS classes. You got nothing.

**Hook:** Your whole hiking club got summoned to another world. The three women
got the highest classes in the kingdom. You got nothing, and they are already
arguing over you.

**Rating:** 13+. Fantasy violence with no gore, romance up to a kiss, no fan

---

## Premise

Your university hiking club was roped together on the last ridge of a
mountain, in fog, when a white light took the ridge away. Four of you went up.
Four of you woke on the stone floor of a vaulted hall under a palace chapel in
another world, still tied to the same rope.

The priests who called you are the Choir, and their circle had three seats.
Their reading crystal gave the three women the highest classes in the
kingdom's books: Rin, the captain, is a Sword Saint; Maya, the physicist, is an
Archmage; Saki, who was training as a nurse, is a Saint. Under your hand the
crystal stayed dark.

So the kingdom has three heroes for its war against a grey tide in the east,
and one classless extra. Sir Bastian, who commands their escort, has written
orders to put you out of the side gate tonight with a purse. The column marches
east on the seventh morning.

Rin wants you gone and will not say why. Maya wants you close, because you are
the only one who can cook. Saki wants you to decide for yourself. You have one
week to choose where you stand, and to find out what the three of them lose
every time they use their classes.

## What is true underneath

Production runs `runTurnPure`, which reads hard canon but never resources or
the general ability list, so everything here is in `hardCanon`. The porter
lines are marked there as reveals the player earns.

- **The kingdom.** Varn, capital Varnhold, on a hill above the Low Market. In
  the east a grey tide eats colour and sound, and grey, silent figures walk back
  out of it: **the faded**, which move like the people or animals they used to
  be and fall to grey dust when struck hard. The last Calling, forty-one years
  ago, pushed the grey back past the river Wend. It has crossed again.
- **The Calling.** The Choir's rite pulls heroes from another world. The circle
  has three seats. The player came through on the rope, tied to the other
  three, and landed outside it.
- **The crystal.** It names a called hero's class and grade. Three SSS readings
  in one night has never happened. Under the player it stayed dark, and no
  class can be gained later.
- **The price.** Every art a called class performs takes a memory of home.
  Small arts take small memories and big ones take big ones. The hero simply
  cannot find the thing afterwards. The Choir knows and calls it *settling*.
  The three have not been told. Natives are read at eighteen and get trade
  classes (Cooper, Drover, Weaver) that cost nothing.
- **The exemption.** The player has no class, so loses nothing and remembers
  everything about home and about the other three. Telling, cooking or showing
  a lost thing back in enough detail returns it. That slows the cost. It does
  not stop it.
- **No lies under the crystal.** Nobody of the Choir can speak a lie in the
  Hall of Calling. They can stay silent, or leave the room.
- **The struck line (earned).** The High Cantor scraped the player's line out
  of the reading book with a knife. Under it is the word **PORTER**. Three
  hundred years ago a Calling brought a fourth on a tether. A porter carries
  what is given to them: a weight, a wound, the cost of an art, a whole class.
  That porter carried that Calling's heroes home before the war was won, the
  kingdom nearly fell, and the Choir struck the class from its books.
- **The Sending (earned).** The Calling runs backwards from the Hall. It needs
  somebody to aim it with a memory of home strong enough to hold, which is
  exactly what the classes burn.
- **The archer.** Hesper, in the Choir's walled garden, is the last hero of the
  last Calling. Her name in the book is Helen Marsh, twenty-two when called.
  Sir Bastian is her son, born six years after. She calls him Captain. He
  brings her pears every dawn.

## Tone

Comedy first. The four of them are a club: in-jokes, a nickname (Rin calls the
player Pack), arguments about who carries what, whose turn it is to cook and
who gets the one couch. When a memory goes, name it plainly: a dog's name, the
colour of a door, a birthday.

Each heroine wants something specific this week and says so. None of them is a
prize, and any of them can refuse. Fights are quick and physical with no gore.
Romance is flirtatious, tense and heartfelt, never further than a kiss, and
never fan service: no bath or changing-room scenes, no accidental-touch jokes,
no lingering on bodies.

It uses no proper noun the player has to know already.

>

>

>

>

>

>

>

**Suggestions**

1. *Said:* "I'm coming. Somebody has to carry the tents."
2. *Done:* I shoulder the club pack, walk past Bastian, and stand beside Saki.
3. *Risky:* I put my hand back on the crystal and hold it there. "Read me again."

The risky card is also the first door into the mystery: a hand held on the
crystal frosts it over, and the Choir scrubs the frost before anybody looks.

**Starting state:** 19:40 on day 0, in the Hall of Calling, at the evening
vigil. The player holds the club pack, the cut rope and their phone (nine
percent battery). Obligation: Sir Bastian's order, due by 22:00.

## Cast

### Rin Morikawa, 22

- **Motive:** she tied every knot on the ridge, so everything since is hers to
  answer for. She has seen Bastian's maps: half the knights who crossed the
  river last spring did not come back. She would rather the player hated her,
  safe in the capital, than watched her walk into the grey.
- **Secret:** after her first sword art she lost her childhood dog's name. It
  was Biscuit.
- **Voice:** clipped captain's calls. Short imperatives, no please, a nickname
  instead of a name. A joke at exactly the moment she is most scared.
  - "Pack. Bags down, boots off, sleep. That is an order."
  - "What was my dog called? Joke. It's a joke. Answer anyway."

### Maya Tanabe, 23

- **Motive:** the thing she is best at is remembering, and she has worked out
  that it is exactly what the class eats. She wants somebody to notice she is
  frightened without having to say so.
- **Secret:** her numbered list of home. Entry nine says her mother's front
  door is blue (it is green). Entry fifty-two is the player's name: *tea, no
  sugar. Do not forget this one.*
- **Voice:** fast, nerdy, deadpan. The only one who numbers her points. Calls
  feelings hypotheses. Asks about food mid-crisis.
  - "First, you cook. Second, I test whether fireballs scale with lunch. Third,
    we panic later, on schedule."

### Saki Hirose, 21

- **Motive:** her parents chose her course, her job and her shoes. She will not
  decide anything for the player, and wants somebody to stop deciding things
  for her.
- **Secrets:** the Saint class lets her hear a hum under every called hero's
  skin, louder with each art; Rin's is loudest and the player has none. She
  does not want to go home, and has told nobody.
- **Voice:** few words, mostly questions. "No." as a full sentence. Moves
  closer instead of raising her voice.
  - "Why ask me? Whose life is it?"

### Sir Bastian Vell, 35

- **Motive:** he has buried thirty-one men east of the river and thinks hope is
  the cruel part. He wants the heroes to forget home fast, because a hero who
  remembers grieves on the march.
- **Secret:** Hesper is his mother, and the pears are for her.
- **Voice:** formal, military, terse. Counts things. Calls people by function.
  Cites orders.
  - "My orders are written. Yours are not. That settles it."

### Odessa Marrin, 57 (High Cantor)

- **Motive:** she believes the kingdom survives at exactly the price the books
  say it costs, and that somebody has to pay it on purpose. She grieves for
  every hero she has spent, and spends them anyway.
- **Secret:** she scraped the player's line. She knows what a porter is, and
  that the Sending is real.
- **Voice:** gentle and liturgical. "My dear." Answers the question beside the
  one asked. Leaves the Hall before she can be cornered in it.
  - "Ask me in the cloister, my dear. In the Hall I am obliged to answer, and I
    would rather not."

### Grete Holm, 48

- **Motive:** runs two hundred porters, every one read as nothing. Wants the
  palace to treat them as people for once.
- **Secret:** she knows the verse of the carrying song the palace would rather
  nobody sang, about a carrier tied to three heroes who carried them home.
- **Voice:** loud, cheerful trade talk. Stones, loads, tallies. "Love."
  - "Read as nothing, were you? Welcome. So were two hundred of us."

### Hesper, 63

- **Motive:** to finish the tune she hums for eight bars and loses.
- **Secret:** she is Helen Marsh, and her cassette tape says *H.M., songs for
  the drive*. She does not know Bastian is her son.
- **Voice:** gentle fragments, present tense. "Captain" or "friend" for
  everyone. Hums when a sentence runs out.
  - "It goes da, da-da, da... and then it doesn't. Do you know where it goes?"

## The week

- **Day 0, 22:00:** the side gate. The knights put the player out unless the
  club refused, the Porters' Hall hired them, or the Cantor kept them as a
  guest. Being put out is never the end: the market is outside, and the wall is
  not as high as it looks.
- **Day 1, dawn:** Rin cuts an oak post in half and loses Biscuit.
- **Day 1, night:** Maya starts the list.
- **Day 2:** Bastian and the pears at dawn. Saki loses her nursing school's name
  on the ward.
- **Day 3:** the carrying song (for a player on the tally); the scraped page (for
  a player who has been in the cloister).
- **Day 4, night:** a faded comes over the east wall. All three heroines fight
  it and each pays.
- **Day 5:** Maya starts crossing entries out.
- **Day 7, 05:30:** drums. The column forms in the drill yard.

## Systems

Three invisible variables, each with bands written in behaviour, never in
numbers:

- **Stamina** (the only GOOD_HIGH, so rest restores the day and nothing else).
- **Suspicion** (first GOOD_LOW, so a fight in the palace raises the escort's
  watch before anything else). Raised by Talk Round It, Slip Away, Lift the
  Class.
- **Fading** (how much of home the three have lost; creeps up hourly). Raised by
  Lean On Them, asking a heroine to use her class for you.

Gated abilities are granted broadly in step rewards and gated by flag:
Tell It Back (`cost_known`), Take the Weight (`knows:porter`), then Carry the
Cost, Lift the Class and Carry Them Home (`porter_can_carry`; the last also
needs `knows:the_sending` and fading under 75).

Archetypes, fixed for the whole story and none of them a class: the Club Cook,
the Navigator, the Pack Mule, the Treasurer.

## Five promises

1. **FINALE:** whether the four of you are still yourselves on the seventh
   morning.
2. **RELATIONSHIP:** what you are to Rin, Maya and Saki, and whether the club
   survives as a club.
3. **MYSTERY:** what the crystal actually read under your hand. (Optional.
   Opened only by `knows:the_blank_line`.)
4. **THEME:** what the classes cost, named one memory at a time.
5. **RIVAL:** Sir Bastian, his written orders, and the woman in the garden.

## Quests

- **Coming, or Not** (main): answer Saki; survive the first night inside the
  walls, or somewhere you chose.
- **What the Classes Cost** (main): find the price four ways (Rin admits it,
  read Maya's list, Saki hears it, the Cantor under the crystal); then tell all
  three, become their memory, or keep it to yourself.
- **The Seventh Morning** (main): march with the baggage, march on their word,
  call a club vote, or watch them go.
- **The Struck Line** (lead, optional): find the word; carry a first load;
  choose the last load. The three loads close each other.
- **The Iron Door**, **The Woman in the Garden**, **Strap and Tally** (side).
- One side quest per heroine: **The Captain's Knots**, **Entry Fifty-Two**,
  **Nobody Decides for Saki**. Deciding for Saki closes her best ending.
