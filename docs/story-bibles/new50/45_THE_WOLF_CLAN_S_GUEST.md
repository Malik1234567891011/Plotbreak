## 1. Genre and one-line fantasy

**Beastfolk reverse-harem romantic comedy, winter survival, light politics.**
Lean: F. Comparable shelf evidence: Wildbound, 298K (PLAN row #45).

> *Lost in the snow. Found by four wolves.*

Card hook: You wake in a fur tent in a wolf clan's winter camp, and the four
brothers who brought you in are arguing about which of them found you first.

## 2. Why the fantasy works

- **Four men want you under their roof, and none of them for the obvious reason.**
  Each brother is motivated, not just voiced. Varek saw your lantern two nights
  early and waited. Sil already knows where you came from and is hiding your
  pack. Ronan needs a guest because a guest is standing, and he needs standing
  before midwinter. Tam carried you, and he is also the thing that walked ahead
  of you in the storm.
- **The law forces the choice in the first minute.** Guest-right says a stranger
  must be claimed by a host, who answers for the guest. A stranger with no host
  goes back into the snow. So the opener is a real choice, and every answer to
  it, including no, lands somewhere.
- **Two clocks, both concrete.** The Company's factor comes up the mountain on
  day two to buy you back with flour. The pass closes at dusk on day four, and
  whichever side of it you are on is where you spend the winter.
- **Cosy is allowed.** Hauling water, losing fishing bets and learning which
  brother takes the last of the tea is a legitimate way to play the whole story.

## 3. Premise (as shipped)

You came north with the last caravan of the year: three sleds, nine people and
a guide, crossing a mountain pass a week before the snow shut it. On the second
night of the storm you took a lantern and walked out to find the guide, and the
guide was gone.

You remember the cold, and something pale walking ahead of you through the
shallowest snow. Then nothing.

You wake in a hide tent in the winter camp of a wolf clan: people with wolf
ears, wolf tails, sharp noses and one old law about strangers. A stranger has to
be claimed by a host, who answers for everything the guest does. A stranger
nobody claims goes back into the snow.

Four brothers brought you in, and each of them says he found you first. Each has
his own reason to want you under his roof. Only the eldest has not said what his
is.

You have three days before the pass closes for the winter. In that time you need
a host, a place at the fire, and an answer for the polite man from the south who
has come up the mountain with a sled of flour to buy you back.

Stay until spring or go down before the snow decides for you. Somebody in this
camp knows exactly what walked ahead of you in the storm, and is hoping you
never ask.

(235 words, six paragraphs, none over 70 words. It never says "white wolf" or
"old shape".)

## 4. Tone and pacing

Cold outside, warm inside, funny in between. Practical kindness: the better
blanket, the warm side of the fire, a fish somebody caught specially and
pretends not to have. The four brothers bicker like brothers and never all
agree.

- Romance is slow, chosen and specific, and lives in what people do, never in
  lingering on bodies. Nothing past a kiss.
- The clan is neither savage nor noble; the south is neither villain nor
  saviour. Both have somebody counting flour.
- Violence is quick, never lingered on, and always has consequences. Brothers
  settle a quarrel by wrestling to the ground in the old form, never with
  blades. The clan does not kill guests; it walks them to the boundary stones.
- When a scene has nothing at stake, keep it cosy and short.
- The face at the top of a beat is whichever brother is talking to the player.

Prose style: `webnovel`. Two style examples ship: Ronan's fishing bet on the ice
(Sil: "He cheats.") and Varek at the lookout ("Ask me that again when the pass
is shut.").

## 5. Player starting authority

- **Who you are:** a BLANK protagonist, an adult traveller who crossed the pass
  for work. The player sets name and pronouns. The opener never genders the
  player: the brothers argue over "the stranger", which is also the law's word.
- **Archetype** ("Why did you come north?", kept for the whole story):

| Archetype | Role | Starts with | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| You Draw Maps | Scout and surveyor | Survey map, Company +10 | You carry the Company's red line through the valley without knowing it |
| You Heal People | Healer and medicine | Herb satchel, Greyhowe +10 | Useful on day one; Sil's natural ally |
| You Carry Letters | Talk, nerve and bargaining | Sealed letter | The Company's offer, unread, addressed to the clan mother |
| You Guard Caravans | Sword and endurance | Caravan sword | You lost the caravan; every wolf-kin knows where your sword is |

- **Setup questions:** name, pronouns, archetype, one true sentence ("What is
  true about you that the cold did not change?"), and two advanced fields: a
  first lean toward one brother, and what the brothers see when you sit up.
- **Can do from turn one:** say it plainly, lie well, watch them, earn your
  keep, tend someone, stand your ground, fight, slip away, let someone close,
  play them off. Two abilities unlock in play: Call on Guest-Right (once you
  have a host) and Follow the White Tracks (once you know what to follow). Both
  are granted by the first quest step and stay locked by flag until then.

## 6. Minimal hard canon

1. Everyone is an adult: Varek 31, Sil 27, Ronan 25, Tam 21, Aud 56, Dagny 26,
   Kell 44. The player is a grown traveller.
2. The Greyhowe are wolf-kin: ears, tails, noses, night eyes. People in every
   respect, never animals.
3. Guest-right: a host ties a red cord on the guest's wrist and answers for the
   guest with his own standing. A stranger nobody claims gets a day's food and a
   walk to the boundary stones.
4. Ronan found the player in a drift, Tam carried the player three miles, Sil
   kept the player breathing through the first night. Varek claims no part.
5. The high pass closes at dusk on day four (world minute 5280). Nobody crosses
   again until thaw.
6. The stores are short. Aud knows the flour runs out a month before thaw.
7. The Company is a southern trading house. Its factor, Matthias Kell, wants the
   player back, wants the survey, and wants the white wolf.
8. Varek saw the player's lantern two nights early and waited to see whether
   Company men followed. He thinks he lost the right to claim the player.
9. Tam can take the old shape, a white wolf, in deep cold. Only Aud and Varek
   know. He led the player through the storm.
10. Wolf-kin smell fear, fever and a nervous lie. A calm lie passes.
11. Violence has consequences rather than detail. Brothers wrestle, never use
    blades. The clan does not kill guests.
12. The cold is the edge of the map. A guest who wanders gets followed by the
    host, usually furious.
13. Romance is slow and chosen: at most a kiss. Nobody takes what is not
    offered. Every brother can be refused without punishment.

## 7. Prologue and opening scene

7:40 on day one, Sil's warm tent. All four brothers are in it, because each has
a schedule block there in the morning. Aud is in her lodge and arrives at 7:52
(`we_aud_at_the_flap`: "Well. Whose?"). The opening obligation gives the player
twelve minutes to name a host before she asks.

The opener as shipped (107 words; Varek speaks at word 55; it ends on a demand):

> "I found the stranger."
>
> "I carried the stranger."
>
> "I kept the stranger breathing. That counts more."
>
> You wake in a fur tent, warm for the first time in days. Four men with wolf ears are arguing over you.
>
> Then you move, and all four go silent.
>
> The eldest kneels beside you, grey ears flat. "Easy. I'm Varek. Ronan found you in the snow. Tam carried you. Sil kept you alive."
>

>
> "That's our mother, and she runs this clan. She'll ask whose guest you are, and a stranger with no host goes back into the snow. Pick who you trust. Quickly."

The only proper nouns are the four brothers, and Varek introduces all of them
before the demand. No place, clan or faction is named.

Three cards, three different moves:

- **Said:** "Varek. You're the only one who didn't claim me. I'll trust you."
- **Done:** I reach for Tam's sleeve and hold on.
- **Risky:** I throw off the furs and make a run for the tent flap.

The spec runs all three through the rule-based parser; none reads as an attack.
Running is a real route (`ran_for_it`): Ronan catches the player at the
treeline, and by custom whoever catches a runner keeps them.

Opening panel alt: four wolf-eared brothers around a fur bed in a firelit hide
tent, the eldest kneeling closest with his grey ears flat, the other three
mid-argument behind him.

## 8. Major cast

Cast order is cover order. Nobody is minor-coded: Tam is "the fourth brother",
never the youngest of anything, and nobody is ever a cub or pup.

### Varek, 31: the eldest

- **Role:** leads the hunters; expected to lead the clan.
- **Appearance:** tall, broad, dark hair with one streak of grey, grey ears
  that go flat when he thinks, heavy grey fur mantle.
- **Want:** get the clan to thaw; get out of the midwinter betrothal without
  costing the clan the pass.
- **Fear:** that waiting is the only thing he is good at.
- **Secrets:** saw the lantern and waited; betrothed to Dagny by his mother;
  has covered for Tam's shape for six winters.
- **Voice:** short declaratives, rarely more than eight words. Opens hard
  sentences with "Listen." Measures time in nights. Never fills a silence.
  *"Mother will ask what you are worth. Give her a number. She respects numbers."*
- **Starts:** trust 25, affection 15, respect 30.

### Ronan, 25: the third brother

- **Role:** best hunter, loudest voice; found the player face-down in a drift.
- **Appearance:** lean, rust-red hair tied back badly, a nick out of his left
  ear, a tail that wags every time he lies.
- **Want:** challenge Varek at the midwinter fire, in the old form, for the
  right to speak for the hunters.
- **Fear:** being the brother people like and nobody trusts.
- **Secrets:** the planned challenge; he broke a Company trapper's arm, and the
  Company wants him handed over.
- **Voice:** fast, loud, opens with "Ha!", turns everything into a bet.
  *"Pick Varek and you get rules. Pick me and you get fun. Pick Sil and you get soup."*
- **Starts:** trust 15, affection 30, respect 10, rivalry 15.

### Sil, 27: the healer

- **Role:** the clan's healer; the player woke in his tent.
- **Appearance:** slim, short black hair, charcoal ears, sleeves rolled, a burn
  scar on one hand.
- **Want:** get the camp through the cough; learn everything the player knows
  about the south.
- **Fear:** a fever he cannot break; his mother learning where his medicine
  comes from.
- **Secrets:** treats Company clerks at the post in exchange for southern
  medicine, and has heard them talk about the road; hid the player's
  Company-stamped pack.
- **Voice:** level and clinical, "Hm.", a dry joke in the same flat tone as the
  diagnosis. *"Drink this. It tastes like boiled bark because it is boiled bark."*
- **Starts:** trust 30, affection 20, respect 25.

### Tam, 21: the fourth brother

- **Role:** carried the player three miles; gave the player his cloak.
- **Appearance:** tall, long-limbed, white-blond hair, silver ears that go pink
  when he is embarrassed, a patched tunic because the player has his cloak.
- **Want:** see the south once; keep the Company's hunters off the pass.
- **Fear:** the Company finding out what walks the pass in a storm.
- **Secrets:** the old shape; a bag packed under the woodpile.
- **Voice:** quick and eager, two questions at once, "sorry", then says it
  anyway. *"Is it true southern towns keep lamps burning all night? Every street?
  Who pays for the oil?"*
- **Starts:** trust 35, affection 35, respect 10.

### Clan Mother Aud, 56 (called Aud)

- **Role:** the brothers' mother and head of the clan; decides whether a guest
  stays.
- **Voice:** brisk and wry; counts in mouths and sacks; calls people "love" or
  "stranger" and switches without warning. *"Every guest is a mouth, love."*
- **Secrets:** the flour runs out a month early; she has half-agreed to Kell's
  flour for the player as a fallback.
- **Starts:** trust 0, affection 0, respect 10.

### Dagny of the Cold Ridge, 26 (called Dagny)

- **Role:** heir of the rival clan, betrothed to Varek, wants out.
- **Voice:** dry and blunt, opens with "Let me save us both some time." Talks
  about romance like trade. *"Cold Ridge has forty hunters and no flour. Greyhowe
  has flour and no hunters. That is the whole romance."*
- **Secret:** Kell made her mother the same offer. Both clans are being bid
  against each other.
- **Starts:** respect 15, rivalry 10.

### Matthias Kell, 44 (called Kell)

- **Role:** Company factor at the foot of the south road; hired the caravan.
- **Voice:** smooth, formal, apologetic; prices, clauses, "regrettably",
  "arrangement". *"Forty sacks for the traveller. Sixty if the papers come too.
  For the white wolf, I would empty the storehouse."*
- **Secrets:** the survey is for a road and a garrison through the winter
  valley; the white-wolf bounty is his own idea.
- **Limit:** will not go above the boundary stones after dark.
- **Starts:** trust 30, affection 5, respect 20.

The spec holds every pair of voices under 30% shared vocabulary and keeps each
verbal tic ("Listen.", "Ha!", "Hm.", "Let me save us both some time",
"Regrettably") to one speaker.

## 11. Keyword and lore book

- **Guest cord:** red braid on the wrist; whose knot it is matters. A grey one
  means kin, and nobody living has seen that done.
- **The old form:** wrestling to the ground inside a ring of the clan. The only
  way brothers fight.
- **The old shape:** a wolf-kin who can become a full wolf in deep cold. Feared
  in the south, bountied by the Company.
- **Right of road:** what the sealed letter asks for. A road means a garrison.
- **The horn:** the clan mother's call. It sounds in the opener.

## 12. The five promises

1. **Which side of the pass you are on when the snow shuts it** (FINALE).
2. **Which brother you trust, and what that costs the other three** (RELATIONSHIP).
3. **Why the eldest did not claim you** (MYSTERY): the lantern.
4. **What the Company actually wants on this mountain** (RIVAL): a road, a
   garrison and a pelt.
5. **What walked ahead of you in the storm** (THEME): Tam, and the den.

## 14. Emergent routes (quests)

- **Whose Guest** (MAIN, starts active). Name Varek, Sil, Ronan or Tam (each
  needs talking to and some trust), or refuse (stand your ground: Aud becomes
  your host), run (Ronan catches you and keeps you), or fight (Aud again, and
  Suspicion climbs). Every answer lands with exactly one host. Then the first
  night: work, truth, a lie, closeness or watching.
- **The Man With the Flour** (MAIN). Learn the offer from Kell, from Sil, or by
  listening at Aud's lodge. At the midwinter fire: a host stands up for you
  (guest-right), you earned your place, you expose the road, you go with Kell,
  or nobody speaks and the clan trades you for flour.
- **What the Survey Is For** (SIDE). The ledger, the map, the letter, Sil or
  Dagny: all reveal the road and garrison.
- **Four Brothers, One Guest** (MAIN). Learn each brother's secret, then choose
  one of them, nobody, or set them against each other.
- **Two Mothers' Bargain** (SIDE). Help Dagny find another price, get Varek to
  refuse, or let it stand.
- **What Walked Ahead of You** (LEAD, optional). Hidden behind
  `knows:the_white_wolf`, which only a watchful, curious or trusted player sets.
  Find the den with Tam or alone; then keep his secret, tell Aud you know, or
  sell it to Kell.
- **Before the Pass Closes** (MAIN). Stay (kept by the clan), go south with a
  brother, go south guided to the stones, go down with the Company, or let the
  snow decide. Both south routes close at minute 5280.
