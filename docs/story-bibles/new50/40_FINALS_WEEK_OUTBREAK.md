# FINALS WEEK OUTBREAK

**Slate:** #40 of the fifty (`docs/stories-50/PLAN.md` §3). Zombie survival, male lean, OOC evidence: OOC High Zombie Apocalypse 1.9M.
**Genre:** campus survival horror, ensemble drama, dark comedy, a little romance
**One-line fantasy:** It is 2 a.m. in the university library during finals week. The sick ones outside have walked into doors all night, and now one of them is trying the handle of your room the way a person with a key would.

## Why this fantasy works

Zombie survival is OOC's proven horror shelf, and a campus is the setting every player already knows: the library at 2 a.m., the vending machine, the stairwell with one fire door that sighs, the exam timetable taped to the front desk. The player does not need any lore to understand the stakes. They need a door, a light and a person who is frightened.

The twist that carries ten turns and more: **the infected are learning.** On Sunday they walked into doors. Tonight they turn handles. At nine in the morning every one of them who was booked for an exam will walk to the exam hall and sit down, because that is their routine. They are not dead, they are classmates, and every door the player holds is a choice about a person.

The second engine is arithmetic. There are six uninfected people in the building and a helicopter coming at half past six with four seats. One of the six has not slept in sixty hours and is hiding why under her sleeve.

## Story bible

It is **Tuesday, 2:07 a.m., finals week**, at a university the county sealed on Monday evening. Phones show signal but calls do not connect. The campus wifi and the students' group chat are still up. The emergency alert reads: *Shelter in place. Do not approach affected persons. Await evacuation instructions.*

It began on **Sunday night at the student health centre**: three students with fevers, no sleep in three days, who stopped answering to their names an hour after arriving. They were volunteers in **ND-12**, a wakefulness compound trialled in Professor Haas's pharmacology lab on forty paid student volunteers. Students called it *the study pill*. Two hundred dollars to stay awake through finals. Three volunteers turned at about seventy hours without sleep and bit people, and it has spread the old way since.

### The Regulars

The group chat calls the infected **Regulars**, because they keep their routines.

- They are **sick living people, not dead ones.** They do not sleep and rarely speak.
- They walk toward **lit screens** and toward **voices they know**.
- They **repeat their old routines.** Odile Fenn, the night librarian, still locks the building at ten past two. A rower still runs the river path. A nurse at the health centre still does her rounds.
- **A bite passes it on.** Fever within the hour, then no sleep, then the quiet, then the turn, about six hours in all.
- **They are relearning**, in the order people learn a building: doors, then stairs, then keys and keycards, then words. A few have started saying a habit phrase. Professor Haas expects names next.
- They can be shoved, outrun, shut out or locked in. **Hurting one is hurting a sick classmate**, and the prose never treats it as anything else. There is no gore on the page, ever.

### The reset

Haas designed an antidote and never told anybody it existed: the **reset dose**, a sedative that forces sleep and stops the turn if it is given before the turn completes. **Twelve doses** sit in a refrigerator in his lab in the science building, across the quad. You reach it over the quad, or through the **steam tunnel** from the library basement. On a Regular who has fully turned it does nothing anyone has measured. Haas believes a stronger version might, and has never tested it.

## Tone and pacing

- Tense, fast, specific. A door handle, a cracked phone screen, a stairwell door that sighs, keys on the other side of the glass.
- Fear comes from what the Regulars are learning, never from wounds. No blood on the page, no gore, no lingering on injury.
- The survivors are funny because they are frightened. Jokes land between scares.
- Violence against a Regular works, is loud, and costs the group something every time.
- Romance is possible between frightened adults: tension, honesty, a kiss at most.
- The face at the top of a beat belongs to whoever reacted to the player most strongly.

## Player starting authority

The player is **twenty-one, a third-year undergraduate** (BLANK protagonist). They author their name, pronouns, look, and one true sentence about themselves. The archetype sets what they are at this university:

- **You Work the Night Desk**: library assistant. Knows the staff lift, the basement and Odile Fenn, who knows them back. Starts able to *Read the Room*.
- **You Study Engineering**: hinges, loads, fasteners. Starts able to *Hold the Door*.
- **You Row for the University**: fit, up at five, knows campus before anyone is awake. Starts able to *Make a Run*.
- **You Argue for Points**: debate society. Starts able to *Talk Them Down*.
- **You Study Nursing**: ward night shifts, fevers are ordinary. Starts able to *Patch Up*.

The player can hide, fight, lie, run, refuse, walk out alone, sell anybody out, or save everybody. Nothing they do is wrong. Some of it is expensive.

## Opening scene

The player is at a long table in the dark third-floor reading room with Nora Lindqvist, a med student who sat down opposite at midnight. Their laptop is the only light on the floor. The door handle across the room turns all the way down, rises, and turns again. Keys jingle. On the other side is Odile Fenn, the night librarian, turned since Monday, doing her 2:10 lock-up round.

What happens next depends entirely on the first move:

- **Go dark:** she locks them in and moves on. They now need Kwame's keys, Lily's route or a window.
- **Hold the door:** she pushes, patiently, then tries her keys, then leaves. The stairwell has heard.
- **Open it:** she is standing there with her keys, in her cardigan, saying the library is closing. She does not attack unless the light is on her or somebody grabs her.
- **Lure her off:** a phone screen thrown down the stacks takes her with it.
- **Fight her:** it works, it is loud, and Nora will tell you exactly what you did to a sixty-three-year-old woman with a fever.

## Major cast

Every character is an adult and the ages are canon.

### Nora Lindqvist, 24
**Role:** fourth-year medical student, sharing your table since midnight. She speaks first.
**Want:** get everybody on this floor to morning.
**Motive under it:** on Sunday night, volunteering at the health centre, she discharged the first ND-12 volunteer with a form that said *exhaustion*. He turned in his dorm an hour later. She needs to save one person tonight to be able to live with it.
**Fear:** being the one who decides who is not worth saving.
**Secret:** she has seen Lily's pupils and her tremor since one o'clock and knows what they mean. She has said nothing because she has nothing to treat it with.
**Voice:** clipped, clinical, numbers first. **Repeats a sentence twice when frightened** ("They don't do that. They don't do that."). Uses a medical word, then translates it unasked. Still half intends to sit her nine o'clock surgery paper, because if she does, the world is still the world.
**Romance:** possible, slow, earned. A kiss at the gate at dawn at most.

### Kwame Mensah, 34
**Role:** night security officer, six years on this building, master keys on his belt and the county on his radio.
**Want:** get Professor Haas and three students onto the roof at 06:30.
**Motive under it:** he has decided to stay with his building. Odile Fenn trained him on his first night. He has not been able to change the locks on her, and he leaves the staff door on the latch.
**Fear:** being the guard who opened the wrong door. Hearing Fenn say his name.
**Secret:** the helicopter, the number four, and that he is not one of the four.
**Voice:** low, even, procedural. Radio words off the radio (copy, negative, say again), times on a twenty-four-hour clock, dry jokes with no change of expression. He knows every door by its sound.

### Lily Tran, 19
**Role:** first-year architecture student who has drawn every floor of this building, the basement and the steam tunnels included.
**Want:** to be useful enough first that somebody remembers her as more than a symptom.
**Motive under it:** she took the study pill for two hundred dollars of rent. She is **Volunteer 31**, sixty hours awake, with a paper band under her left sleeve. She believes she is going to turn, and she believes falling asleep is what ends her. She has it backwards.
**Fear:** waking up as one of them with her sketchbook still in her hand.
**Boundary:** will not be sedated without saying yes first, and will not be voted on in the third person.
**Voice:** fast, deadpan, structural metaphors (load-bearing, egress, cantilever). Jokes before anyone else can be sad, and goes very quiet when somebody is kind to her. Lies about how many energy drinks she has had.

### Declan Shaw, 23
**Role:** second-year law student barricaded in a third-floor study room with a whiteboard over the glass and a rule for everything.
**Want:** get on whatever leaves first, and keep anyone infected out of the group.
**Motive under it:** at eleven last night he held the stairwell door shut on his study partner **Priya Nair** (22) while she was bitten on the other side. She is a Regular on the second floor now, highlighter still behind her ear, waiting for the lift. Every hard line he draws tonight is there to prove he was right to.
**Secret:** his father's car is in the faculty lot by the science building, keys in his pocket. A car holds five.
**Voice:** formal, recitative, moot court: the rule, the exception, the burden of proof. "For the record." Uses full names when angry. He has read the emergency alert forty times and recites it.

### Professor Konrad Haas, 61 (called Haas)
**Role:** professor of pharmacology, locked in Special Collections on the fourth floor with a grey binder he will not put down. The helicopter is coming for him by name.
**Want:** reach the helicopter with the binder, and get the reset doses to someone who will use them.
**Motive under it:** ND-12 was his compound and his trial. He wants to undo it more than he wants to be forgiven, and is not sure he has the courage to do either.
**Fear:** the binder reaching the administration before it reaches anyone honest. Walking across the quad past forty faces he recruited.
**Secrets:** the trial. The twelve reset doses. His untested belief that a stronger reset could bring back the ones already turned.
**Voice:** measured, academic, courteous, office-hours manner. Answers a question with a better question. Uses the passive voice for anything that was his fault ("Mistakes were made in the dosing schedule. I am aware of how that sentence sounds.").

### Named Regulars (not cast; no lines of their own)
- **Odile Fenn, 63**, night librarian for thirty-one years. Locks up at 2:10. Says the library is closing. By the small hours, she says Kwame's name.
- **Priya Nair, 22**, contract law, Declan's study partner. Second-floor landing, waiting for a lift, pressing the button.

## Factions

1. **The Group Chat**: about two hundred survivors across campus posting which stairwells are clear, who is bitten and who has water. They remember who helped. (First in array order: public violence costs standing here first.)
2. **The Cordon**: county emergency response. Gate, tent, helicopter, an incident commander who speaks in times. Wants the uninfected out by noon, Haas out at 06:30, and nothing that walks leaving.
3. **The Administration**: what is left of university leadership, emailing from a hotel across the river. They know about the trial and would like nobody else to.

## Five promises

1. **The way out (finale):** who is still in this library at noon. Seeded by a handle that turns like a person with a key; paid off on a roof, at a gate or in an exam hall, when somebody says out loud who is going.
2. **Lily's left sleeve (relationship):** she pulls it down every time she reaches for anything. Paid off by a paper band reading VOL 31 and twelve doses across the quad.
3. **The study pill (mystery):** why three healthy volunteers walked into the health centre on Sunday. Seeded by a grey binder Haas will not put down; paid off by ND-12, forty volunteers, seventy hours.
4. **They are learning (the antagonist):** on Sunday they walked into doors. Paid off at nine, when they walk to their exams, and when one of them says a name.
5. **Not enough seats (theme):** Kwame's radio and the message he has not passed on. Paid off by four seats, six people, and the person who does the arithmetic out loud.

## Quests

- **The Handle** (main, active): get through the next minute, then find out who else is in the building.
- **Four Seats** (main): find out what Kwame is not saying, then decide who is on the roof at 06:30.
- **Volunteer 31** (main): find out what is wrong with Lily, fetch the reset doses, and put her to sleep before she stops being Lily, if she says yes.
- **The Study Pill** (lead, optional): find out what Haas did, then decide what happens to the binder: the Cordon, the group chat, or the fire.
- **Doors, Then Stairs** (side): learn the Regulars' routine, then use it: walk among them, cross the empty quad at nine, or lock them into the exam hall.

## Endings

Eleven, all reachable, every rarity, real losses among them. The storyteller reads twelve at most.

- **Everyone Walks Out** (rare): Lily dosed and asleep, the helicopter refused, all six across the empty quad at nine.
- **Four Seats** (common): you were on the helicopter. Somebody was not.
- **The Fifth Person** (uncommon): you gave your seat away and watched it lift.
- **Twenty-One Days** (common): still on campus at noon. Sealed in with whoever stayed.
- **Volunteer 31** (uncommon, loss): nobody reached the doses in time, or she said no. At two in the afternoon Lily stops making jokes.
- **Lights Out in the Exam Hall** (unique): the Regulars locked in the exam hall, the binder and the doses in honest hands, and the newest-turned start, one by one, to sleep.
- **The Study Pill** (rare): the binder posted to the group chat. The university cannot bury it.
- **Nobody's Fault** (rare): the binder burned for Haas. The trial stays a "fast-moving viral illness".
- **Same Table, Next Term** (rare): you and Nora, at the gate at dawn, one kiss, and a seat saved at her table in the spring.
- **Office Hours** (unique): Haas stays behind after the seal, dosing the newly bitten at the health centre.
- **On Your Own** (uncommon): you walked out on all of them, early, and nobody saw which way you went.
