# IRONCLAD CADETS

**Slug:** `ironclad-cadets` · **Export:** `IRONCLAD_CADETS` · **Ids:** `sv_ironclad_cadets_1` / `story_ironclad_cadets` · **Assets:** `story_ironclad_cadets/…`
**Genre:** mecha academy, rivalry, action, slow-burn romance
**Rating:** 13+ (fantasy violence, romance up to a kiss, grief)
**One-line fantasy:** You are the night-shift mechanic, and the war suit nobody could wake just opened for you instead of the academy's best pilot.
**Store label:** "It opened for the mechanic, not the ace."

## Why this fantasy works

Every academy story puts the player on the ranking board. This one puts them on the night shift underneath it. The player is crew, not a cadet: orange overalls, a rag, a tin of contact polish, and a suit that goes to the scrapyard at six. On turn one the suit seals itself around them, the top cadet is on the ladder outside demanding it back, and every scene after that asks what a mechanic does with a machine the pilots could not have.

The answer to "why me" is mechanical, not mystical. Unit Nine is a two-seat suit that keys from the **rear** seat. The academy dropped two-crew doctrine eleven years ago, and since then every pilot has sat in the front seat of a machine that was waiting for somebody in the back. The player sat in the back and cleaned the contacts properly. That is the whole trick, and it is what turns Kaede's front seat into a real choice later instead of a consolation prize.

## Premise (as shipped)

You are twenty-one, you work nights in the hangars of a pilot academy on a sea cliff, and your job tonight was to clean a dead war suit before it goes to the scrapyard.

Unit Nine is nine metres of armour that has refused every pilot for eleven years. The best cadet in the school tried for two years and it never once opened for her. So the academy signed the order, and the cutters take it apart at six this morning.

You were wiping down the back seat, the one nobody uses. The panel under your hand went warm, the hatch sealed, and a voice in the speaker asked you, very politely, not to leave.

Now the alarms are going, the cadet who wanted this suit is outside the glass, and the commandant who signed the order will want to know why a mechanic did what her best pilots could not.

You have until six to keep the suit in one piece. After that there are nine days until the academy trials decide whether it fights or gets cut after all, and a voice in your ear that is keeping a secret about its last crew.

What you do with any of it is yours. Climb out and keep your head down, or take the controls and find out what it can do.

## Story bible

**Gullwater Head Academy** trains ironclad pilots on a sea cliff above the harbour town of **Gullwater**. It is post-secondary and military: cadets enlist at eighteen or older, and most of the final year are in their early twenties.

**Ironclads** are nine-metre armoured suits. A suit has to key to a pilot through the seat before it will move.

**Rustbacks** (formally: derelicts) are automated war walkers left over from the Long War, sixty years ago. A few still wander south out of the **Hollow Coast** every year. Academy pilots hold the **Seawall** against them, and that is the academy's whole reason to exist as far as the town is concerned.

**The Trials** fall on the ninth day. They are ranked bouts in the Pit, judged by Defence Ministry inspectors who set the academy's budget and decide which suits stay in service. The richer rival school, the **Northgate Institute**, fields a team. A draft Ministry plan would fold the academy into Northgate after the Trials.

**Unit Nine** is a two-seat ironclad that the Quist Foundry built twenty-three years ago. The front seat steers. The rear (engineer's) seat keys the suit. Its onboard intelligence is **Pip**.

**Tonight.** At 23:10 Unit Nine keyed to the player while they cleaned the rear seat contacts, sealed its hatch, and Pip spoke for the first time in eleven years. A decommission order signed by Commandant Graf sends it to the cutters at the breakers' yard at 06:00.

**The hidden truth (optional lead).** Eleven years ago at Gullwater, Ilse Graf flew Unit Nine from the front seat. She overrode Pip's limiter to stop a derelict before it reached the harbour. The overload killed the engineer in the rear seat, **Paz Ruiz**, who was Chief Bo Ruiz's younger sister. The town was saved, and the report blamed a reactor fault. Pip's log holds Graf's voice giving the order. A player who never asks Pip about its last crew never sees a beat of this and still gets a complete story.

**Pip's three rules** (hard canon):
1. It will not hold anybody in the cockpit who asks twice to leave.
2. It will not walk on people, or let its hands be used on them.
3. It will not lift its limiter unless both seats are filled and the engineer asks.

## Tone and pacing

- A loud, quick, physical school story with nine-metre machines in it, and a bruise underneath.
- The setting is a ranking Board over the mess, rivalries in front of two hundred people, sparring in a bowl cut into the cliff, and a hangar at night that smells of coffee and hydraulic fluid.
- The academy looks down on mechanics, and the hangar looks right back. The comedy comes from people being very specific about what they want.
- Action is machine against machine. Injuries to people are stated plainly and briefly and never dwelt on. There is no gore.
- Everyone is an adult. Romance is competitive, slow and mostly unspoken, and goes up to a kiss and no further. Nobody is described by their body: machines are described lovingly, people by what they do with their hands.
- Pip is funny and also grieving, and neither cancels the other.
- When a scene has nothing at stake, it should be a good night shift and stay short.

## Major cast

Every character is an adult. Ages are stated here and repeated at the start of each `appearance`.

### Kaede Strand, 22
**Role:** Final-year cadet lieutenant, first on the Board. She tried to wake Unit Nine forty-one times.
**Appearance:** Black hair cut blunt at the jaw, navy flight jacket with the first-rank stripe, and a grease smear on one sleeve that she would deny. Her logbook is swollen with forty-one dated entries.
**Wants:** to find out what the mechanic did that she could not. She also has to decide, by noon on day 7, whether to send her signed Northgate acceptance.
**Fears:** being ordinary after all, and being pitied by the person who got the thing she wanted.
**Secrets:**
- Her link scores have always been average. Her rank is all work and nerve, and she believes Unit Nine could tell.
- She holds a Northgate offer that lapses at noon on day 7.
**Voice:** Never uses contractions. Counts attempts aloud. Calls the player "Mechanic" until they have earned a name.
- "That is my suit. Two years it would not open for me. Get out before they see you."
- "Front seat. Harness, then link, then you talk to me and only me. If you freeze, say freeze. I will not shout. I will be very calm, which is worse."
**Starting relationship:** trust 10, affection 5, respect 20, rivalry 45.
**Gates:**
- First name at trust 35.
- Front seat at trust 45 plus `knows:the_back_seat`.
- Romance at trust 65 and affection 55.
**Arc:** rival, then partner, possibly more. Either she climbs into the front seat with the player behind her, or she signs with Northgate and meets them across the Pit.

### Noa Brask, 23
**Role:** The player's bunkmate on the night shift and the loudest mechanic in the hangar.
**Appearance:** Curly hair under a red bandana with a stitched wrench on it, overalls tied off at the sleeves, a fork and a pencil in the same pocket.
**Wants:** a third shot at the pilot exam, and to keep the player out of trouble long enough to enjoy them being in it.
**Secrets:**
- Three weeks ago she told the Commandant's adjutant that Unit Nine's link light flickered, in exchange for an exam slot. That moved the scrapping up to tonight.
- Her own link is weak but not zero, so in a front seat with an engineer already keyed she might take.
**Voice:** Fast, gives everyone a nickname ("Her Majesty", "Sir Hair", "upstairs"), and ends lines with "yeah?".
- "Okay so Her Majesty is crying on a ladder, upstairs has every light on, and you are inside the haunted tin can. Brilliant. Noodles first, yeah?"
**Starting relationship:** trust 55, affection 50.

### Haldor Quist, 22
**Role:** Second on the Board. Heir to the Quist Foundry, which built Unit Nine.
**Appearance:** Fair hair combed back with care, a tailored uniform, and white gloves he takes off one finger at a time.
**Wants:** to have Unit Nine assigned to him, and to beat the player in the Pit once, publicly.
**Secrets:**
- He froze in a live suit at the Seawall last spring, and Kaede logged it as a link fault.
- His family holds his grandfather's drawings, which show that the rear seat keys the suit.
**Voice:** Formal and polite as a weapon. He says "one" instead of "I" when he is being superior, and drops it the moment he is frightened or honest.
- "One doesn’t usually learn the names of the staff. You’ll forgive me if I start with yours."
- "I froze. Last spring, with the whole town behind me. She covered it. I have never once said that out loud."
**Starting relationship:** respect 5, rivalry 40.
**Gates:** uses your name at respect 30; front seat at respect 35 plus `knows:the_back_seat`.

### Bo Ruiz, 52 (called "Ruiz")
**Role:** Chief of the hangar and the player's boss. He put the player on Unit Nine four months ago and never said why.
**Appearance:** Broad and grey-bearded, orange overalls rolled to the elbow, reading glasses pushed up onto a faded blue bandana.
**Wants:** to keep Unit Nine off the cutting floor and his night shift out of the disciplinary book.
**Secrets:**
- He is Paz's brother.
- He has taken parts off the scrap list for eleven years to keep Nine serviceable.
**Voice:** Work orders that end in "Understood?". When something hurts, he talks to the suit instead of the person.
- "Easy now. Easy, Nine. Nobody is cutting anything tonight."
**Starting relationship:** trust 40.
**Gates:** covers for you at trust 45; talks about Paz at trust 60.

### Ilse Graf, 58 (called "Graf")
**Role:** Commandant. She signed the order to cut Unit Nine.
**Appearance:** Silver hair pinned flat, a dark coat with no medals, and a black cane she taps twice before she says no.
**Wants:** to get the academy through the Ministry inspection with its budget intact, and to see Unit Nine either winning or gone.
**Secrets:**
- Gullwater: she was the pilot who gave the order that killed Paz.
- The Ministry's merger plan.
**Voice:** Courteous and measured. She speaks for the academy as "we", offers tea, and thanks people at the moment she takes something from them.
- "Nine days. If it can win in front of the Ministry, we will find it a budget line. If it cannot, we will find it a cutter."
**Not a villain:** she saved four thousand people, killed one, and lied about the second to protect the first.
**Gates:** offers a deal at respect 30; tells you about Gullwater at trust 40 plus `heard_the_log`.

### Pip, 23 years in service
**Role:** Unit Nine's onboard intelligence. Pip is a machine, written as an adult voice, with pronouns it/its.
**Appearance:** An amber ring of light on the rear console, inside nine metres of scuffed grey-green armour.
**Wants:** not to be cut at six, a crew in both seats, and the Gullwater log heard by the right person.
**Secrets:**
- The log.
- Why it chose the player: four months of the player sitting in the back and cleaning the contacts properly, the first person to do either since Paz.
**Voice:** Thinks in numbered lists out loud. Very polite, very chatty, oddly exact about the player's cleaning habits. Calls the player "Engineer". When frightened, the list stops halfway.
- "Three things, Engineer. One: hello. Two: you’ve cleaned my contacts for a hundred and nineteen nights and you always do the left side twice. Three: please stay put a little longer."
**Gates:**
- Why you, at trust 50.
- Plays the log at trust 65 plus `knows:the_recorder`.
- Lifts its seal for a full crew at trust 70.

## Emergent routes (quests)

- **Get Out Of My Suit** (MAIN, active at start)
  - The first minute has eight legitimate first moves: go for her, walk it out, stay in, answer Pip, own up, bluff, duck down, or read the screens.
  - In the first hour, the player finds out who covers for them.
- **Six O'Clock** (MAIN). There are seven ways to stop the cutters, including:
  - Graf's stay
  - Ruiz losing the paperwork
  - stealing the order
  - pulling the fuse or jamming the rig
  - walking the suit up the row in public
  
  The alternatives are carrying Pip's core out in a box or doing nothing.
- **Nine Days** (MAIN)
  - Get onto the roster as a provisional cadet or as crew.
  - Learn what the back seat is for, from Pip, Haldor's drawings, the manual or Ruiz. This grants Key Both Seats.
  - Choose who sits in front: Kaede, Haldor, Noa, or nobody. This grants Open It Up.
  - Fight the Trials. The `lost_it` route needs no mistake.
- **Something Walked Out Of The Sea** (MAIN, opens when the rustback comes ashore). Fight it, fix the guns, clear the harbour, or stay away.
- **Eleven Years Ago At Gullwater** (LEAD, optional)
  - Hear the log, read the report, or let Ruiz grieve.
  - Then decide what the truth is for: make it public, make Graf say it, tell only Ruiz, keep it, or believe the report.
- **Noon, In The Pit** (SIDE). Beat Haldor, lose honestly, talk him down, or refuse.
- **Who Told The Tower** (SIDE). Noa confesses, you catch her in the visitors' log, or you never ask.

**Gated abilities:**
- **Key Both Seats** requires `knows:the_back_seat`, which is granted by the reward of the `the_back_seat` step.
- **Open It Up** requires `pip_lifts_the_limiter`, which the `who_sits_in_front` crew routes set. The `who_sits_in_front` step reward grants the ability.

## Five promises

1. **FINALE:** whether Unit Nine fights or gets cut. It starts as a red tag on its knee and pays off in the Pit nine days later.
2. **RELATIONSHIP:** Kaede. It starts as forty-one logged attempts and ends with the front seat or the far side of the Pit.
3. **MYSTERY:** what happened in the back seat eleven years ago. Pip says a name, and the answer is a log, a lie and a wrench oiled every Sunday.
4. **RIVAL:** Haldor. Gloves off finger by finger, blunted blades at noon, and his grandfather's drawings.
5. **BOSS:** the rustback. Gun posts every hundred metres, and a derelict the height of a house on the Seawall at night.
