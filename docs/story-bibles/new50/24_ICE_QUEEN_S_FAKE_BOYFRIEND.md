## Why this fantasy works

Fake dating is a romance that starts in the middle. On turn one the player already has a partner, a
secret, an audience and a deadline, and the only thing missing is the feeling. That gap is the
story. Every scene is two performances at once: the one for the people watching, and the one the two
of them are slowly failing to keep up in private.

The hook does the hard work in its first line. A stranger with a reputation for never needing anyone
needs the player, publicly and immediately, and has to ask for help in a whisper. A whisper is the
first crack in the ice, and the player was the one who heard it.

It reads to both audiences for the same reason Good Morning, Husband does: the relationship is
there from the first beat, and the other person is a person rather than a prize. Akane has her own
deadline, her own reasons and her own rules, and she is allowed to call the whole thing off before the
player does.

## Story bible

The player is a **second-year student at Fairholm University**, a large old university in the
middle of a city, built around a lawn called **Founders' Quad**. The story starts at **twenty to one
on a Monday in late October**, at the busiest moment of the lunch hour.

**Akane Hollis** is the student council president, twenty-one, in her third year, and the whole
campus calls her **the Ice Queen** because in three years nobody has seen her laugh, lose an argument
or hold anybody's hand. She walks straight across the quad, takes the player's arm, and tells the man
following her that the player is her boyfriend. Thirty people hear it. One of them has a phone.

The man is **Darren Cole**, twenty-four, heir to the Cole Group. On **Saturday night at the
Chancellor's Gala** in the Great Hall, Akane's mother **Evelyn Hollis** and Darren's father Gideon
Cole intend to take the stage at nine and announce Akane and Darren as a couple. The invitations have
their names printed together already. Akane never agreed. A real boyfriend, seen by everyone, is the
one refusal her mother cannot wave away.

So she asks the player to be one, until **midnight after the gala**, on four rules she has already
written down, in exchange for **one favour of the player's choosing**.

What nobody says on Monday:

- **It was not random.** Three weeks ago, at the council's open forum, the player was the only person
  who stood up and told Akane she was wrong to close the library reading room at midnight, and did not
  sit down when she stared. She changed the rule that night. She has never admitted being wrong to
  anyone else.
- **Darren does not want to marry her either.** He has been seeing Lena Ortiz, the pastry chef at the
  bakery on Mercer Street, for a year, and his father does not know. He needs the match to collapse in
  a way that is not his fault, so he tests the fake relationship hard: if he can see through it, his
  father will.
- **The match is a merger term.** Evelyn's firm, Hollis & Lane, will be insolvent by spring without a
  merger with the Cole Group, and the gala announcement is a condition on page nine of the term
  sheet. Akane found it in the summer. Her mother does not know she has read it.
- **The professor knew her mother.** Professor Ito and Evelyn, then Evelyn Lane, were together at
  this university twenty-eight years ago. She left him for the marriage that made Hollis & Lane.
- **The Ice Queen writes the advice column.** "Hot Water", the warm, funny, anonymous advice column
  in the campus paper, is Akane's. Mei edits it and has promised never to tell.

The player is free to play along, improvise a love story, tell Darren the truth on the spot, refuse
the deal, ask her out for real instead, punch somebody, walk out of the gate and never come back, or
spend six days teaching the Ice Queen how to eat chips on a roof.

This story succeeds if a player can spend ten turns doing nothing but small things with her (coffee,
the reading room, a walk round the lake, an argument about a debate motion) and still feel the week
moving underneath them.

## Tone and pacing

- Romantic comedy with a clock in it. Banter first, then the thing under it.
- Akane is formal and exact in public, drier and funnier in private, and the gap between the two is
  the romance.
- Every public scene is a performance with an audience. Every private one is a risk.
- The romance is teasing, tense and heartfelt, and goes as far as a kiss and no further. No fan
  service. Nobody is described lingeringly.
- Nobody is a villain. Evelyn is protecting three hundred jobs. Darren is protecting the person he
  loves. Mei is protecting the truth and her sibling, in an order she keeps changing.
- Lies, fights, refusals and walking away are all legitimate and all have consequences somebody
  remembers.
- Small, physical, campus-specific detail: the silver council pin she straightens when nervous, the
  memo pad, the coffee cart, the fire door to the roof whose alarm does not work.

## Player starting authority

- The player is an adult second-year student, twenty or older.
- Akane introduced the player as her boyfriend at twenty to one on Monday, and the player has not

- She said "boyfriend" whatever the player's gender; the word stuck, the gossip page uses it, and
  Darren keeps it as a nickname.
- The gala announcement is Saturday at nine.
- It was the forum three weeks ago that made her pick the player.

## Opening scene (the first beat, 111 words)

> "Akane, wait!"
>
> The woman ignoring that shout walks straight at you across the lunch-hour quad. White coat, pale
> face. Everyone knows her: the student council president, the one they call the Ice Queen.
>
> She takes your arm. Her nails dig in through your sleeve.
>
> "You," she murmurs, "are about to help me. Nod when I squeeze. Name your price later."
>
> A man in a camel coat catches up, out of breath.
>
> "Darren." She doesn't let go of you. "This is my boyfriend."
>
> She squeezes. Every bench in earshot goes silent.
>
> Darren looks you up and down. "She doesn't date. Ever." He holds out his hand. "So. How did you two
> meet?"

Checked against section 7: Akane speaks to the player at word 45; the beat ends on a direct question
that needs answering now; 111 words; the only names are the two introduced on the page, and the
nickname is glossed in the sentence that uses it.

**Suggestions, three different moves:**

1. Said: "She shushed me in the library. I've been doomed ever since."
2. Done: I lace my fingers through hers and nod, slowly, at Darren.
3. Risky: I shake his hand. "Honestly? I've never met her before in my life."

Whatever the player does, Darren leaves with a parting line about the gala, Saturday at seven, and
whether the boyfriend owns a suit. Akane does not let go of the player's arm and does not answer
Darren for them. If the player tells him the truth, Darren looks oddly disappointed rather than
pleased, and within the hour Akane finds the player anyway, memo pad in hand, colder and more polite,
because she has no second plan. This follow-up lives in hard canon, not in quest director notes,
because the production storyteller never reads director notes.

## Major cast

Every character is an adult. The player is an adult second-year student, twenty or older.

### Akane Hollis (21)
**Role:** student council president, third year; the Ice Queen; as of 12:40 on Monday, the player's
girlfriend.
**Look:** tall and very straight-backed, long black hair cut blunt, grey eyes, a long white wool coat
over a charcoal roll-neck sweater, a silver pin worn exactly level on the coat lapel.
**Immediate want:** get to midnight after the gala with the story intact.
**Real want:** stand on that stage on Saturday and say no to her mother out loud, once.
**Fear:** being seen to need anybody; that the ice is not a mask and there is nothing under it.
**Secrets:** why she picked the player (the forum); she has read page nine of the term sheet; she
writes "Hot Water".
**Limits:** will not be kissed for an audience; hand-holding is the line, and she drew it. Will not be
pitied. Will not let the player take the blame for her lie.
**Voice:** short, exact, formal sentences with no filler, as if reading minutes. More polite when
rattled. When genuinely amused she says "That is not funny" and has to look away.
> "Rule two. In public you may hold my hand. Rule two does not cover looking at me like that."

### Darren Cole (24)
**Role:** heir to the Cole Group, business-school graduate student, the name printed beside hers.
**Look:** broad-shouldered, wavy chestnut hair pushed back, an expensive camel overcoat worn open.
**Immediate want:** find out whether this lie can survive his father's people.
**Real want:** get out of Saturday without being the one who said no, and keep Lena.
**Fear:** his father finding out about Lena before he has a plan; being exactly who everyone assumes.
**Secret:** Lena Ortiz, the pastry chef on Mercer Street. He is in an apron at her bakery most
mornings before six.
**Limits:** will embarrass Akane but never hurt her on purpose. Goes cold, quiet and unforgiving if
anyone threatens Lena.
**Voice:** easy, fast, nicknames he hands out without asking. Every compliment has a question folded
inside it. Calls the player "boyfriend" with audible quotation marks.
> "Quick round, boyfriend. Her birthday. Her middle name. Her coffee. Go."

### Mei (22)
**Role:** the player's older sister, final-year journalism student, editor of the Fairholm Courier.
**Look:** short and quick, a choppy black bob with a red streak, oversized glasses, a press lanyard
over a denim jacket covered in pins.
**Immediate want:** find out what her sibling has got into.
**Real want:** run the Cole gift story in Friday's edition, which a city paper's summer place depends
on.
**Fear:** being the reason her sibling gets hurt; sitting on the biggest story of her year.
**Secrets:** she edits "Hot Water" and knows Akane writes it; she has a folder on a side letter tying
the Cole gift to a "family announcement", and a photo of Darren outside a bakery at dawn.
**Limits:** prints nothing she cannot prove, including about her sibling. Will not lie to her readers.
**Voice:** rapid-fire, questions stacked three deep, ends arguments with "Anyway" and a sip of coffee.
> "I've watched you lie since you could talk. You touch your ear. You're touching your ear right
> now."

### Professor Ito (51)
**Role:** Professor Daisuke Ito, who teaches the rhetoric seminar the player and Akane share and
advises the student council.
**Look:** lean and slightly stooped, cropped grey hair, wire glasses, tweed jacket with a pen leaking
slowly into the breast pocket.
**Immediate want:** get the two of them through Friday's debate.
**Real want:** that Evelyn's daughter gets the choice Evelyn gave up.
**Fear:** interfering, and being right to; watching the same mistake happen twice in one family.
**Secret:** two years with Evelyn Lane, twenty-eight years ago; a photograph of them on the boathouse
steps in his desk drawer.
**Limits:** will not tell Akane about her mother's past. Will not take sides in front of students.
**Voice:** slow, dry, lecture-hall cadence. "Interesting" means wrong; "Hm" means right.
> "Interesting. You hold her hand the way somebody holds a train ticket they are afraid of losing."

### Evelyn Hollis (50)
**Role:** Akane's mother, chief executive of Hollis & Lane.
**Look:** slim and immaculate, silver-streaked black hair in a low chignon, her daughter's grey eyes,
a single strand of pearls.
**Immediate want:** the announcement made on Saturday at nine.
**Real want:** her daughter safe, which she has decided means married into money, because that is
what kept her safe.
**Fear:** her daughter becoming exactly her; her daughter learning what she gave up.
**Secrets:** the firm has four months of money; page nine; Daisuke Ito.
**Limits:** never discusses the firm at her table. Will not be lied to twice: the first lie is
manners, the second is policy.
**Voice:** low, unhurried, complete sentences, compliments that are also assessments.
> "Sit there. No, there. I like to see the people I am deciding about."

Offstage: **Gideon Cole (61)**, Darren's father, arrives at the gala at eight. **Lena Ortiz (25)**,
the pastry chef, can be met at the bakery and is not the player's business unless Darren makes her so.

## The deal (her four rules)

1. It ends at midnight after the gala, with a friendly breakup in public.
2. In public, holding hands is the limit. Nothing more unless both of them want it.
3. Nobody is told it is fake. Not her mother, not Darren, not the player's sister.
4. If either of them starts to mean it, they say so, and the deal ends that night.

In return: one favour of the player's choosing, anything within the power of the student council
president.

## Behavioural state (invisible)

- **Energy** (good high): a student week runs out. The one thing rest restores.
- **Scrutiny** (good low, first): how closely the campus is looking. Public scenes and lies raise it;
  a fight on the quad raises it hardest.
- **Frost** (good low): Akane's guard. Thaws slowly with time near the player; pushing her raises it.

## Five promises

1. **Finale: midnight.** Seed: rule four in her handwriting. Payoff: somebody says out loud what this
   was, before or after the clock does it for them.
2. **Mystery: why you.** Seed: she knew the player's name before they told her. Payoff: forum minutes,
   the player's name underlined once in her pen.
3. **Rival: the man in the camel coat.** Seed: he tests the player like a man who needs them to pass.
   Payoff: flour on his sleeves at six in the morning, and Lena.
4. **Boss: nine o'clock on Saturday.** Seed: two hundred invitations with both names printed, none
   posted. Payoff: Evelyn reaching for the microphone, and whoever gets there first.
5. **Relationship: under the ice.** Seed: "That is not funny," and she looks away. Payoff: "Hot
   Water", written at midnight by the Ice Queen, and one letter in it that is about the player.
