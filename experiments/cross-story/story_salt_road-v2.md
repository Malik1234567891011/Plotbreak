## Turn 3 — typed

**Player:** I ask what is really on the other end of this road.

> You ask what is really waiting at the far end of eleven days’ white road.

> **Ferrow.** Coast.

> Ferrow checks the stopper on the nearest skin, then pulls the knot firm enough to squeak leather.

> **Ferrow.** Salt docks. Nets. Warehouses. People who pay porters to ask fewer questions than they have.

> **Sabe.** That is not really an answer.

> **Ferrow.** It is the answer I have. I was hired to guide a case to the coast, not to know its destination.

> Her hand closes around her map case. For the first time, the flat certainty in her voice has a seam in it.

> **Ferrow.** But I intend to see who receives it. If we arrive.

> Sabe shifts their pack and looks from Ferrow to the locked brass-cornered case. The gate throws a last narrow bar of shade across the road; beyond it, there is nowhere to hide and no answer waiting close enough to reach.

> **Sabe.** So we are all carrying something we would rather not explain.

> **Ferrow.** Five minutes became three. Decide what stays.

## Turn 5 — tapped

**Player:** I shoulder the case and step onto the salt road.

> You shoulder the case and step past Ossun’s last wall. The salt takes the imprint of your boot for half a second, then its hard white crust gives nothing back. Behind you, vendors call to other travellers; ahead, heat already trembles over a road with no landmarks but the thin black stroke of Ferrow’s back.

> **Ferrow.** Drink when I say. Not when the sun tells you to.

> Sabe falls in on your other side, their pack riding too high and knocking softly against their shoulders. By noon, the gate has vanished behind the glare. Conversation dries up before the water does. Ferrow keeps the pace unkind but possible, counting under her breath whenever she checks the sun.

> Six hours after leaving Ossun, a dark circle resolves from the flats: the first well. Its stone ring sits in a trampled basin, its windlass creaking over a deep, cold sound. Ferrow stops at its edge rather than hurrying in.

> **Ferrow.** First well. We fill everything. We rest forty minutes. Then we walk until the light goes.

> **Sabe.** Good. I have been thinking very sincerely about becoming part of the scenery.

> There are other footprints around the well, some fresh, some filled with windblown salt. The case has rubbed a hot ache into your shoulder, but it is still locked, still with you.

## Turn 6 — tapped

**Player:** I spend the next few days following Ferrow’s rationing exactly and keeping the case close.

> For three days, you follow Ferrow’s rationing exactly. A mouthful at dawn. A measured swallow in the furnace of noon. Another when the light begins to thin. The case stays within reach when you sleep, its brass corners wrapped in your spare cloth against the salt; its weight becomes a permanent bruise under your shoulder.

> Sabe learns to tighten their pack, stops asking how far, and saves their breath for the walking. Ferrow’s pace never softens, but neither does she ask more of you than she takes herself.

> On the third afternoon, the Second Well rises out of the glare: a broad stone mouth crowded with travellers, tethered packs, and the scrape of buckets. It is the last certain water before the road begins charging interest. You fill every skin until the leather swells dark and heavy.

> **Oren.** That is Ferrow, then. I was beginning to think you had taken the northern line.

> A sunburnt man with a well-made pack stands beside the windlass, one hand resting on a full skin. He looks first at Ferrow, then at the case, quickly enough to be polite and slowly enough that you notice.

> **Ferrow.** Oren. You are late.

> **Oren.** A day. The sun and I disagreed about direction. I am heading coastward, if you have room for another set of hands.

> **Ferrow.** One more mouth. State what you carry.

> **Oren.** Six days’ water after filling here. Food for eight. A compass that is mostly honest. No injuries. No debts that followed me this far.

> Ferrow looks at the filled skins, then at the flat horizon beyond the well. Her arithmetic does not visibly change, but she does not answer him yet.

> **Ferrow.** Your call. You carry the case. You decide whether he walks with us.
