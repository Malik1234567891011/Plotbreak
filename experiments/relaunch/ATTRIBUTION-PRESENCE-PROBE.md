## A1-sabo — Sabo, two-hander

**Player:** I ask Sabo straight out whether he actually plans to stay on this island.

*expected:* Sabo speaks; his lines are dialogue blocks

> You catch Sabo halfway up a muddy slope, one hand hooked around a root and his pipe tucked under his arm. Luffy is below, trying to climb the same slope by charging it headfirst and sliding back down each time.

> You ask Sabo straight out whether he actually plans to stay on this island.

> **Sabo.** "No."

> The answer comes so quickly that even Luffy stops scrambling. Sabo looks past you through the trees, toward the blue strip of sea that only shows between branches when the wind shoves them aside.

> **Sabo.** "I mean, not forever. We get the money, we get a boat, and we leave. That was always the plan."

> He gives the brim of his top hat a hard tug. Too hard. His grin shows the gap where his tooth is missing, but it does not reach his eyes.

> **Sabo.** "But if you mean soon—"

> **Sabo.** "Look. There’s something wrong down at Gray Terminal. Men have been walking around with lists. And my family’s been asking questions in High Town again."

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** "Lists are bad."

> Luffy nods fiercely at his own conclusion, then loses his grip and lands on his backside in the mud.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** "I hate lists."

> **Sabo.** "That is not a reason, Luffy."

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** "It is."

> Sabo huffs a laugh despite himself. Then he looks at you again, waiting to see whether you will call him scared, whether you will tell him to stop watching the city that threw him away.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo · location: mt_colubo · Day 1 · morning*

## A2-luffy — Luffy, loud and interrupting

**Player:** I tell Luffy he is too small to come with us and I mean it.

*expected:* Luffy speaks, probably several times

> You stop on the steep dirt track hard enough that Sabo, a few paces ahead, turns around. Luffy nearly walks into your back. The forest is already loud with insects; somewhere uphill, a monkey shrieks like it has been personally insulted by the sun.

> You face the little idiot in the straw hat and tell him straight: "You’re too small to come with us. I mean it. Go home."  ⚠️ UNATTRIBUTED-SPEECH?

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** No.

> He says it at once. No sulk, no wobble. He plants his sandals in the mud and stares up at you, chin lifted under the oversized brim.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I’m coming, Ace.

> **Sabo.** Look, that was unusually clear. I’m impressed. It also changed absolutely nothing, apparently.

> Sabo leans against a tree, pipe over one shoulder, watching Luffy with the wary interest usually reserved for traps. Luffy takes one quick step closer, close enough that if you move he will have to move too.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I’m small now. I’ll get big. Then I come.

> He has not understood the part where you mean today, or tomorrow, or ever. But he understood that you meant it. For the first time this morning, his grin is gone.

> **Sabo.** We’re heading toward the treehouse. If he follows, he follows. You can throw him in a river after we get there, if it cheers you up.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** Don’t throw me in the river!

> **Sabo.** See? He’s learning.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo · location: mt_colubo · Day 1 · morning*

## A3-dadan — Dadan, shouting

**Player:** I tell Dadan we are not coming back tonight and she can do what she likes about it.

*expected:* Dadan shouts; every shout attributed

> You plant both hands on the table and tell Dadan, "Sabo and I aren’t coming back tonight. Do whatever you want about it."  ⚠️ UNATTRIBUTED-SPEECH?

> **Curly Dadan.** OH, I’LL DO WHAT I LIKE, ALL RIGHT! I’ll lock the door, eat your share, and let the mountain cats teach you manners!

> The ladle comes down with a wet smack beside the stew pot. Dadan’s face is red enough to match the fire—then her eyes flick once to the windows, to the thick green mountain outside, and her grip tightens around the handle.

> **Monkey D. Garp.** Bwahahaha! Spending the night out, are you? Fine training for Marines!

> Garp is sprawled at the far end of the table with Dadan’s largest bowl in one hand and half a loaf in the other. He must have slipped in while everyone was looking at the door; nobody hears the man arrive until he is already eating their food. His Marine coat is thrown over the bench, enormous and bright against the smoky room.

> **Curly Dadan.** YOU! Don’t encourage them! And stop eating, you uniformed plague!

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I’m coming!

> Luffy springs to his feet on the bench, immediately loses balance, and catches the tabletop with both hands. He grins at you from under the tilted straw hat as if your declaration settled the only important question in the world.

> **Sabo.** We haven’t actually said where we’re going yet.

> **Monkey D. Garp.** Doesn’t matter! Forest, ravine, tiger den—everywhere builds character! Especially tiger dens! Bwahaha!

> **Curly Dadan.** If either of you comes back missing a limb, don’t expect sympathy. I’ll have to cook extra, you idiots.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo, Curly Dadan, Monkey D. Garp · location: dadan_house · Day 1 · morning*

## A4-garp — Garp, arriving

**Player:** I stand my ground and wait to see who it is.

*expected:* Garp speaks on arrival

> You plant your feet on the root-knuckled path and keep the lead pipe low at your side. The enormous crashing comes closer, uprooting a shrub in a spray of dirt.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** Grandpa! You came!

> Luffy launches himself downhill before anybody can stop him. A broad hand catches him one-handed by the back of his vest, stopping him inches from a muddy slope.

> **Monkey D. Garp.** You got taller! Terrible news. Now you’ll eat even more! Bwahaha!

> Garp swings Luffy in a loose circle, deposits him on his feet, and strides into the clearing with the confidence of a man who has never once worried whether he was invited. His Marine coat is open, his sack smells strongly of smoked meat, and his grin is aimed straight at you.

> **Sabo.** Look, I’m sure this is a perfectly ordinary family visit, but why does he sound like an avalanche?

> **Monkey D. Garp.** Because avalanches don’t get promoted, blondie. You—boy. Good stance. Marines could use that stance.

> He says it like an offhand compliment, then closes the distance in three long steps and raises one fist over your head. Not swinging yet. Just waiting to see whether you flinch.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo, Monkey D. Garp · location: mt_colubo · Day 1 · morning*

## A5-mixed — Four-way argument

**Player:** I tell all of them at once that I am leaving this island one day and none of them can stop me.

*expected:* multiple named speakers in one beat

> You plant the end of your pipe against the floorboards and say it loud enough to cover Garp’s laugh, Dadan’s shouting, and Luffy’s chewing all at once: you are leaving this island one day, and none of them can stop you.

> **Curly Dadan.** Oh, listen to him! Ten years old and already announcing his departure like anybody asked for a speech, you arrogant little rat.

> **Sabo.** He’s right about one part. Nobody can stop him forever. The rest of that was unnecessarily dramatic.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I’m leaving too! I’m going with Ace!

> **Monkey D. Garp.** No, you’re not. Both of you are joining the Marines. Problem solved! Bwahaha!

> Garp laughs, but it runs out early. His hand closes around the bowl until the wood creaks under his thumb. Dadan catches the change; her ladle lowers by an inch.

> **Monkey D. Garp.** The sea doesn’t care how tough you think you are, boy. It takes loud kids, quiet kids, strong kids. Then it sends back a story people tell wrong.

> For one strange second, nobody eats. Then Luffy climbs onto the bench, thrusting the bread at your chest with both hands.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I’ll be strong! Then the sea can’t take us!

> **Curly Dadan.** Sit down before you fall in the soup, straw hat.

> Dadan turns sharply toward the pot as if it has personally offended her. Garp looks at you from across the table, grin gone now, waiting for an answer he plainly does not know how to ask for.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo, Curly Dadan, Monkey D. Garp · location: dadan_house · Day 1 · morning*

## A6-offscreen — Known speaker offscreen

**Player:** I stay up here and ignore whoever is shouting at me from the bottom of the tree.

*expected:* the shouter is known — should still be attributed

> You sit back from the hatch and refuse to look down. The treehouse creaks around you in the warm morning wind; somewhere below the platform, bark gets kicked with determined little thumps.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** Ace! I’m still climbing!

> **Sabo.** He is. I regret to report that he has improved by nearly an inch.

> Sabo swings himself through the side opening and drops onto the planks beside you. He has brought half a stolen bun wrapped in paper, already missing more than half of it. Below, Luffy’s fingers appear at the rim of the hatch—then vanish with a yelp and a violent shower of leaves. He lands on a lower branch rather than the ground, hugging it like it betrayed him.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I got higher!

> **Sabo.** An excellent way to measure a defeat.

> Luffy immediately starts hauling himself up again. Sabo watches him for a second, then looks at you with the particular expression he wears when he has reached a conclusion and expects you to dislike it.

> **Sabo.** Look, he’ll either get up here, fall until he learns how not to, or follow us home and yell outside Dadan’s window. Those are the available outcomes.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I’ll get up there!

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo · location: asl_treehouse · Day 1 · morning*

## A7-unknown — Genuinely unknown speaker

**Player:** I freeze when a voice I do not recognise says something behind me in the dark.

*expected:* speakerId null is CORRECT here

> You stop so hard your pipe knocks against your knee. Sabo goes still on the branch above, one hand closing around his own pipe. Luffy, halfway through squeezing past you, bumps into your back and makes a small offended noise.

> Three children creeping around after dark. Either you’re lost, or you’re about to become somebody’s problem.

> The voice comes from behind a ridge of collapsed wardrobes and rusted stove doors. You cannot see its owner—only the orange pinprick of a cigarette, low to the ground, then a boot scraping broken glass. The lantern crews keep moving at the eastern edge. Tin cans clink. A sharp chemical smell drifts through the rubbish heaps.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** We’re not lost!

> **Sabo.** Luffy.

> Too late. The cigarette ember shifts closer. Sabo drops soundlessly from the branch beside you, but he does not raise his pipe. His eyes are on the dark, calculating exits that are not there.

> Then you’d better know what those men are carrying. Lamp oil. Enough to make this whole place burn.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo · location: gray_terminal · Day 1 · night*

## P1-stays-behind — Somebody stays put while the player goes

**Player:** I leave Sabo up at the treehouse and walk down to the shore on my own.

*expected:* Sabo NOT present

> **Sabo.** Fine by me. I’ll count the money again while you go brood at the ocean like an elderly fisherman.

> Sabo swings himself back through the treehouse hatch, already digging for the little tally marks he keeps on a plank. You head downhill before he can add another word. The mountain path bites at your sandals, all exposed roots and wet stones; the forest thins by degrees until salt wind cuts through the hot green smell.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** Ace! I’m coming!

> You do not turn around. Behind you, there is a crash, an offended bird squawk, then the rapid slap of small feet scrambling after yours. Of course there is.

> By the time you reach Dawn Island Shore, Luffy is panting at your elbow, straw hat crooked over one eye. Grey sand runs to the rickety jetty. Beyond it, the sea is huge and bright and empty enough to make the mountain feel like a fist unclenching.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** You said on your own. But I’m not part of you. So I can come.

> He plants both hands on his hips, pleased with this logic. A gull drops something silver onto the jetty planks and shrieks at both of you.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy · location: dawn_shore · Day 1 · morning*

## P2-exits — Somebody leaves mid-beat

**Player:** I tell Luffy to go back to Dadan’s and I watch him until he is out of sight.

*expected:* Luffy NOT present

> You stop on the steep dirt path and point downhill, toward the smoke-thread above Dadan’s roof.

> “Go back to Dadan’s. Now. Don’t follow us.”  ⚠️ UNATTRIBUTED-SPEECH?

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** No.

> He says it immediately, small hands hooked under the straps of his straw hat. Then he turns around anyway—because you told him to go back—and starts marching downhill with furious little stomps.

> **Sabo.** That worked far better than I expected.

> You fold your arms and watch. Luffy reaches the bend where the path vanishes behind a thick clump of ferns. He does not look back. For one clean second, he is gone.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I’m back!

> His head pops straight up from behind the ferns. He has apparently walked three steps off the path, crouched, and decided that counts. A grin splits his face.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** Now I’m coming!

> He scrambles uphill toward you again, sandals skidding on loose dirt. Sabo puts a hand over his mouth, failing not to laugh.

> **Sabo.** Technically, Ace, you did watch him until he was out of sight.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo · location: mt_colubo · Day 1 · morning*

## P3-arrives — Somebody arrives mid-beat

**Player:** I wait where I am until whoever it is gets here.

*expected:* the arrival IS present

> You stay planted on the root, lead pipe loose at your side, and wait. Whoever it was has already arrived: Dadan stomps the remaining few steps uphill until she is close enough to blot out the path behind her. Ash drops from her cigarette onto her boot. Sabo has the decency to look only mildly caught.

> **Curly Dadan.** Well? What are you waiting for, an invitation written in gold? The stew is getting cold, and I am not cooking twice because you brats decided roots are furniture.

> Luffy rises from his terrible hiding place behind your leg. He looks up at Dadan without a flicker of caution, then points at the axe.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** Are you gonna hit Ace?

## P4-discussed — Absent character discussed

**Player:** I ask Sabo what he thinks Dadan would say if she knew about the can.

*expected:* Dadan NOT present

> You and Sabo are crouched on the broad root of a kapok tree, both of you staring downhill at the treehouse like it might grow ears. Morning heat is already steaming off the leaves. Thirty feet below, Luffy is attempting to climb the same trunk by hugging it and kicking angrily at bark.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I’m climbing! Don’t look!

> He immediately looks up at you while saying it, loses both sandals, and catches himself on a root.

> You ask Sabo what he thinks Dadan would say if she knew about the can.

> **Sabo.** First she’d shout loud enough to knock fruit out of the trees. Then she’d take it away, count it, accuse us of stealing it badly, and hide it somewhere only she knows.

> **Sabo.** Then she’d spend exactly none of it, because she’d decide it was ours. Which is why she absolutely cannot know.

> Sabo flicks a beetle off his sleeve and grins. His pipe rests across his knees; his eyes keep travelling past the treehouse toward the strip of blue sky beyond the mountain.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** What can?

> Luffy has somehow made it halfway up while nobody was watching. He hooks both elbows over the root beside you, chin flat on the wood, beaming like he has won a contest nobody announced.

> **Sabo.** A can full of dangerous, boring grown-up secrets.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I want dangerous boring secrets!

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo · location: mt_colubo · Day 1 · morning*

## P5-nearby — Visible but not in the scene

**Player:** I sit up here and watch the bandits moving around down in the yard.

*expected:* bandits/Dadan NOT present — they are below

> You sit with your boots braced against the ridge, watching the house yard wake up below. The bandits move through it in their usual loud, crooked pattern: one drags two water buckets toward the stream; another comes out chewing dried meat and immediately gets shoved back inside by somebody with a broom. A pair of them wrestle a crate of bottles away from the wall before it can slide into the mud.

Nothing looks like an army. Nothing looks like a fire. That almost makes the thin smoke over Gray Terminal worse—too far away to smell, too steady to ignore.

Luffy flops onto his stomach beside you and watches the yard with fierce concentration. After a few seconds, he points at the bandit with the buckets.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** That one’s losing. The buckets are stronger.

> Sabo snorts, then folds the oil-marking notice into a narrow square and tucks it away. He has not stopped looking toward the Terminal. Below, Dadan kicks open the front door with a cooking pot in one hand and a cigarette clenched between her teeth.

> **Curly Dadan.** BREAKFAST! I SWEAR TO GOD, IF I HAVE TO CALL YOU TWICE, I’M FEEDING IT TO THE DOGS!

> There are no dogs. Luffy pops upright anyway, suddenly urgent. Sabo’s gaze flicks from the house to you, waiting to see whether you are going down for food or turning toward the smoke.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy, Sabo · location: mt_colubo · Day 1 · morning*

## P6-split — The group splits

**Player:** I send Sabo around the long way and take Luffy with me up the short climb.

*expected:* Luffy present, Sabo NOT

> **Sabo.** The long way is where the boar nests, you know. Fine. I’ll meet you at the treehouse before you manage to throw him off a cliff.

> Sabo slips downhill through the ferns, pipe tucked under one arm, taking the broad path that loops around the ridge. You turn toward the short climb: thirty feet of wet root, broken stone, and a narrow shelf that only counts as a path if you refuse to look down.

Luffy brightens as though you have handed him a medal. He immediately plants both hands on the first root, boots scrabbling for purchase beneath his straw hat.

You take the lead up the rock face. Behind you, Luffy grunts, slips, catches himself by wrapping both arms around a root, then hauls himself back onto the slope with a face full of leaves.

> **Monkey D. Luffy.** I’m coming, Ace!

> A vine snaps somewhere above. The next handhold is a slick knot of roots beside a drop into thorn bushes—not far enough to kill either of you, probably, but more than far enough for Luffy to make it everybody’s problem. He is already reaching for it.

*present: Monkey D. Luffy · location: mt_colubo · Day 1 · morning*
